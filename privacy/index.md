# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

MartechSignal runs without accounts, without login walls, and without ads. That design choice does most of the work of a long privacy policy, but here is what you should know.

## What we collect

Nothing about you as a person. The site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics that build profiles. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no heatmaps, no session recording. We can't show you a report on "users like you" because we don't have one.

Server logs exist because any web server keeps request logs for security and debugging: IP address, requested URL, user agent, timestamp. Cloudflare retains these under its standard settings, uses them to catch abuse and debug outages, and neither profiles readers with them nor sells them to advertisers.

## Third-party requests

The pages load no third-party resources for reading: fonts, styles and scripts are served from martechsignal.com itself. The only other requests count page views without cookies or profiles: Umami, which we run ourselves at analytics.martechsignal.com, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, from Cloudflare, which also serves the site.

Vendor links (the "Visit" buttons on tool reviews) go directly to the vendor's website. We don't insert redirect trackers, so we never know whether you clicked, and we couldn't tie it to you if we did.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to the weekly newsletter, we store your email address, and your first name if you give it, for one purpose: sending you the newsletter. Subscribing takes two steps: you submit the form, then confirm from the email we send you. We keep proof of that consent: when you signed up and confirmed, which form you used, and a fingerprint of the sentence shown next to it.

The newsletter runs on software we host ourselves (Agentic Marketing), so your address stays on our own server rather than with a newsletter company. Emails are delivered through Brevo, which passes them on to your inbox and tells us whether each one was delivered or bounced. Our own software doesn't track opens or clicks, but Brevo adds its own: a tiny image that records when an email is opened, and links that pass through Brevo's servers to record clicks before taking you to the page. We don't use that data, and we're moving to a delivery service without this tracking; this page will say so when we have. Links to our own pages carry UTM tags (for example `utm_source=newsletter`), so our analytics can count visits from the newsletter without knowing who you are.

Every issue has an unsubscribe link that removes you at once, and your email client's own unsubscribe button works too. If you sign up but never confirm, your address is deleted after 30 days. Your email is never sold or shared.
