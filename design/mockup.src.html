Verdict

The strongest overall score in this batch: owned automation with code-level control and a real AI agent surface. The cost is maintenance, a learning curve, and a fair-code licence that is not open source in the OSI sense.

What n8n is good at

n8n sits between Zapier-style point-and-click automation and writing your own glue code. You build workflows visually, but any node can drop into JavaScript or Python, and the whole thing can run on your own server.
