## Mailchimp vs Brevo (2026): pricing, multichannel, verdict

Pick Mailchimp if you want a broad commerce-flavored suite with strong brand recognition, from $13/mo. Pick Brevo if your list is large but rarely sent, with paid from $9/mo.

Mailchimp and Brevo end up on the same shortlist when the mailing list arrives before the budget does. Mailchimp is the most recognized name in email marketing, with over 11 million users, an Intuit-owned platform spanning landing pages, social ads, SMS, automation, and a basic CRM. Brevo (born as Sendinblue) prices by monthly email volume instead of contact count and folds email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat, and a light CRM into one account.

Most decisions here come down to how it bills. The figures quoted are the catalog's last verified numbers, each dated on the tool page. This page is desk research rather than a hands-on test.

The choice usually lands on one question: does your list talk often, or just exist. Mailchimp charges per contact and rewards rarely-sent lists badly; Brevo charges per send and keeps 300 free emails a day flowing. Store-heavy teams still lean Mailchimp's commerce suite; multichannel-heavy teams on modest budgets lean Brevo.

## Positioning

**Mailchimp:** Mailchimp is the most recognized name in email marketing, serving over 11 million users from solopreneurs to mid-market businesses. Founded in 2001 in Atlanta and acquired by Intuit in 2021 for $12 billion, it has expanded into landing pages, social ads, postcards, SMS, automation, and a basic CRM.

**Brevo:** Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is a Paris-based multichannel marketing platform that folds email, SMS, WhatsApp, web push, live chat, chatbot, and phone into one contact universe, with a light CRM layered on for pipelines and tasks. Founded in 2012, renamed Brevo in 2023.

## Pricing

**Mailchimp:** Free plan (500 contacts, 1,000 emails/mo); Essentials $13/mo; Standard $20/mo; Premium $350/mo.

**Brevo:** Free plan (300 emails/day); Starter from $9/mo at 5,000 emails; Standard $18/mo at 5,000; Professional $499/mo; Enterprise custom; SMS credits sold separately (figures per 2026-09-29 third-party receipt; vendor pricing page renders price numbers only via JavaScript).

## Deployment and self-hosting

**Mailchimp:** Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by plan tier and contact blocks.

**Brevo:** Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by email volume plus channel credits.

## AI features

**Mailchimp:** 5 documented AI features, including AI content optimizer, AI subject line helper, predictive demographics, AI send-time optimization, and creative assistant.

**Brevo:** 2 documented AI features: AI content generator (drafts subject lines and email copy, adjusts tone) and AI send-time optimization on Standard and up. Brevo also ships an MCP server for managing campaigns and contacts from ChatGPT or Claude.

## Integrations

**Mailchimp:** 8 listed integrations, including Shopify, WooCommerce, Salesforce, Zapier, and WordPress.

**Brevo:** 8 listed integrations, including Shopify, WordPress, Stripe, Zapier, Salesforce, WooCommerce, Google Analytics, and Slack; the directory lists 150+ connectors.

## Lock-in and exit cost

**Mailchimp:** Mailchimp exports contacts, templates, and campaign reports. Automations rebuild elsewhere, and the Intuit bundle discount only matters while the whole stack is used.

**Brevo:** Brevo's lock-in lives in the multichannel data: chat transcripts, WhatsApp threads, and SMS history do not migrate cleanly to single-channel rivals. Contact lists export cleanly; the omnichannel context does not.

## Deliverability and sending

**Mailchimp:** Mailchimp's sending covers the general case well: list hygiene tooling, authentication defaults, and deliverability guidance aimed at mixed B2C lists. High-volume ecommerce bursts are supported but not the design center.

**Brevo:** Brevo's sending is volume-metered rather than burst-tuned: dedicated IP options arrive above Professional, transactional email rides SMTP relay and API on the same account, and deliverability tooling emphasizes daily-limit pacing and list hygiene.

## Decision notes

**Mailchimp:** Pick Mailchimp if your team wants a broad commerce-flavored marketing suite with strong brand recognition, starting at $13/mo for Essentials (free to 500 contacts).

**Brevo:** Pick Brevo if your list is large but rarely sent, or you need one multichannel account on a tight budget, starting free at 300 emails/day with paid from $9/mo.

## Migration cost

Moving lists is the easy afternoon. Mailchimp automations do not export into Brevo workflows, so every welcome series and re-engagement path gets rebuilt and re-verified, and template styling has to be redone in the new editor.

Deliverability deserves its own line in the plan. Consent records and suppression lists have to travel with the contacts, and sending reputation does not. Warm the new setup gradually instead of importing the whole list and mailing it on day one.

Going the other way, Brevo imports Mailchimp lists cleanly, but the meter changes shape: plan by email volume from the sender calendar, not by contacts, and watch the 300/day cap on the free tier while testing.

## When neither is the right answer

Neither fits a team whose core need is ecommerce-native revenue flows and predictive segments: that is Klaviyo territory. And if the whole send calendar is a monthly newsletter under 2,000 contacts, the free tiers overlap enough that switching saves nothing.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Mailchimp if:** your team wants a broad commerce-flavored marketing suite with strong brand recognition, starting at $13/mo for Essentials (free to 500 contacts).
- **Pick Brevo if:** your list is large but rarely sent, or you need one multichannel account on a tight budget, starting free at 300 emails/day with paid from $9/mo.

## Which one is cheaper for a big list that sends rarely?

Brevo, almost always. Mailchimp bills the contact count every month; Brevo bills the sends. A 10,000-contact list mailed twice a month stays cheap on Brevo Starter while Mailchimp charges for every stored contact.

## Which platform handles multichannel out of the box?

Brevo folds SMS, WhatsApp, push, chat, and a light CRM into the same account on every plan tier; Mailchimp spreads channels across add-ons and separate Intuit products.

## Do both run the sending for the team?

Yes. Both are hosted platforms the vendor runs, so the buyer compares billing shape and channel depth rather than infrastructure. Neither asks the team to maintain servers.
