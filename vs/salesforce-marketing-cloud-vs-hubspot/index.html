Positioning

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: Salesforce Marketing Cloud is now branded Agentforce Marketing, and the product line has been rebuilt around agents rather than renamed in passing. The current structure splits by motion: Marketing Cloud Next is the flagship, sold as Growth and Advanced editions per org; the B2C Agentic Marketing Platform carries the legacy Engagement (formerly ExactTarget) install base; B2B runs on Account Engagement, the former Pardot, or the Account Engagement+ edition; and Data 360 is the customer data platform underneath. It was founded in 1999.

HubSpot Marketing Hub: Built around a free CRM, HubSpot Marketing Hub combines email marketing, landing pages, lead capture, campaign management, and marketing automation in a single platform. It is designed for small businesses, growing marketing teams, and enterprises that want to connect website activity, contact records, and revenue reporting without assembling multiple point tools. It was founded in 2006.

Pricing

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: enterprise pricing starts at $1500/mo, and per-org list pricing: Marketing Cloud Next Growth $1,500/mo, Advanced $3,250/mo, both billed annually; Starter $25/user/mo; add-ons Personalization $8K, Marketing Intelligence $10K, Loyalty Management $20K per month (verified 2026-09-06).

HubSpot Marketing Hub: It starts free, and free CRM included; Marketing Hub Starter $20/mo, Professional $890/mo, Enterprise $3,600/mo (verified 2026-08-28).

Deployment and self-hosting

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by seats, tasks, or usage.

HubSpot Marketing Hub: Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by seats, tasks, or usage.

AI features

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: 5 documented AI features, including agentforce campaign creation, agentforce personalization decisioning, agentforce paid media optimization, ai campaign summaries, and agentforce loyalty promotion creation.

HubSpot Marketing Hub: 5 documented AI features, including ai content assistant, predictive lead scoring, ai email subject lines, chatbot builder, and ai campaign recommendations.

Integrations

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: 9 listed integrations, including salesforce crm, data 360 (data cloud), slack, tableau, and mulesoft.

HubSpot Marketing Hub: 8 listed integrations, including salesforce, slack, zapier, shopify, and wordpress.

Decision notes

Salesforce Marketing Cloud: Pick Salesforce Marketing Cloud if you want a hosted platform the vendor runs for you, and agentforce campaign creation and agentforce personalization decisioning matters to your team, starting at $1500/mo.

HubSpot Marketing Hub: Pick HubSpot Marketing Hub if you want a hosted platform the vendor runs for you, and ai content assistant and predictive lead scoring matters to your team, starting free.
