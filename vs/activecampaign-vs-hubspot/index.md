# ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot CRM (2026): pricing, automation

## ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot CRM (2026): pricing, automation

Pick ActiveCampaign if you want deep multi-step automation in one SMB-priced platform, from $15/mo. Pick HubSpot CRM if you want a free CRM with sales, service and marketing on one record.

ActiveCampaign and HubSpot CRM end up on the same shortlist when automation outgrows the email tool. ActiveCampaign combines marketing automation, email, and a light CRM in one platform for small and mid-sized businesses that want enterprise-level automation without enterprise complexity. HubSpot CRM brings sales, service, and marketing workflows into one platform around a unified contact record, with a free CRM that on-ramps teams into paid hubs.

Most decisions here come down to how it bills. The figures quoted are the catalog's last verified numbers, each dated on the tool page. This page is desk research rather than a hands-on test.

The choice usually lands on one question: which system owns the contact. ActiveCampaign prices automation depth per tier starting at $15/mo. HubSpot prices seats per hub, free to start, with the bill arriving as the team and the contact database grow.

## Positioning

**ActiveCampaign:** ActiveCampaign combines marketing automation, email marketing, and CRM in one platform built for small and mid-sized businesses that want enterprise-level automation without enterprise complexity. Founded in 2003 and headquartered in Chicago, it serves over 150,000 customers worldwide.

**HubSpot CRM:** Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, HubSpot CRM brings sales, service, and marketing workflows into one platform around a unified contact record, with pipeline management, deal tracking, email logging, and campaign reporting.

## Pricing

**ActiveCampaign:** Starter $15/mo, Plus $49/mo, Professional $79/mo, Enterprise $145/mo; 14-day free trial.

**HubSpot CRM:** Free CRM forever; Sales Hub Starter $15/seat/mo billed annually ($20 month-to-month); Professional $100/seat/mo; Enterprise $150/seat/mo.

## Deployment and self-hosting

**ActiveCampaign:** Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by plan tier.

**HubSpot CRM:** Hosted only. The vendor runs the infrastructure and bills by seats and hubs.

## AI features

**ActiveCampaign:** 5 documented AI features, including AI content generation, predictive sending, AI win probability, AI subject line suggestions, and smart automation.

**HubSpot CRM:** 5 documented AI features, including AI email writer, predictive lead scoring, AI call transcription, AI meeting scheduler, and AI content suggestions.

## Integrations

**ActiveCampaign:** 8 listed integrations, including Shopify, Salesforce, Slack, Zapier, and WooCommerce.

**HubSpot CRM:** 8 listed integrations, including Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Zapier, and Salesforce.

## Lock-in and exit cost

**ActiveCampaign:** ActiveCampaign exports contacts and campaign reports. Automations, lead scoring, and tracking setup stay behind. List ownership is clear and the CRM data is standard fields.

**HubSpot CRM:** HubSpot's lock-in is breadth: pipeline, tickets, workflows, and reporting all reference the same record, so leaving means re-platforming several functions at once. The free CRM makes entry easy and exit planning easy to postpone.

## Deliverability and sending

**ActiveCampaign:** ActiveCampaign's sending is built around its automation heritage: dedicated IP add-ons, warm-up guidance and strong transactional options. Mixed marketing and transactional is a first-class case.

**HubSpot CRM:** HubSpot's sending inherits the platform tradeoff: solid deliverability tooling inside an ecosystem that wants the whole workflow. Dedicated IPs and deliverability consulting arrive on the higher tiers.

## Decision notes

**ActiveCampaign:** Pick ActiveCampaign if you want deep multi-step automation in one SMB-priced platform, starting at $15/mo on Starter.

**HubSpot CRM:** Pick HubSpot CRM if you want a free CRM to start with sales, service, and marketing on one record, with paid hubs from around $20/mo.

## Migration cost

Moving lists is the easy afternoon. ActiveCampaign automations do not export into HubSpot workflows, so every multi-step sequence gets rebuilt and re-verified, and lead scoring rules get re-expressed in the new vocabulary.

Deliverability deserves its own line in the plan. Consent records and suppression lists have to travel with the contacts, and sending reputation does not. Warm the new setup gradually instead of importing the whole database and mailing it on day one.

Going the other way, HubSpot exports contacts and timeline activity cleanly, but workflow enrollment history flattens. Rebuild the three sequences that earn their keep first: new-lead follow-up, trial nurture, and re-engagement.

## When neither is the right answer

Neither fits a team whose core need is ecommerce messaging driven by purchase data: that is Klaviyo territory. And below a few thousand contacts with simple newsletters, both are heavier than the job.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick ActiveCampaign if:** you want deep multi-step automation in one SMB-priced platform, starting at $15/mo on Starter.
- **Pick HubSpot CRM if:** you want a free CRM to start with sales, service, and marketing on one record, with paid hubs from around $20/mo.

## Which one fits automation-first, and which fits platform-first?

ActiveCampaign for teams that want the deepest automation per dollar in one SMB platform. HubSpot for teams standardizing sales, service, and marketing on one contact record with room to grow into paid hubs.

## Do both run the stack for the team?

Yes. Both are hosted platforms the vendor runs, so the buyer compares automation depth and pricing shape rather than infrastructure. Neither asks the team to maintain servers.
