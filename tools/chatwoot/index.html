Chatwoot

Open-source customer engagement platform with AI-powered chatbot and helpdesk

Open-Source Tools · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Built as an open-source customer engagement platform, Chatwoot brings messaging, live chat, and helpdesk workflows into a single interface for teams that want control over their customer data. It supports conversations across channels such as website chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, and email, while integrations with Slack, Zapier, Shopify, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Google Analytics help connect support activity to broader marketing and revenue systems. The platform is aimed at startups, SaaS companies, ecommerce teams, and developers who need a flexible alternative to closed support suites. Chatwoot's main differentiator is its self-hostable MIT-licensed codebase, backed by an active GitHub project with more than 34,000 stars. This gives organizations the option to run it free on their own infrastructure or use paid cloud plans, including Hacker at $19 per month, Startups at $49 per month, and custom Enterprise pricing. Its AI features include an AI chatbot, conversation summaries, auto-replies, agent assist, and sentiment analysis, making it useful for routing, triage, and agent productivity. An API also supports custom extensions and product integrations. Compared with commercial alternatives such as Intercom, Zendesk, or Freshdesk, Chatwoot trades some polish and managed convenience for transparency, customization, and lower entry cost. It is best for technical marketing, support, and RevOps teams that want an open-source helpdesk with AI-assisted conversations and the flexibility to self-host or scale on cloud plans.
