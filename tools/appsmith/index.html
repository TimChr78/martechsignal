Appsmith Review

Open-source platform for building admin panels and internal dashboards on your existing databases and APIs

Workflow Automation · Free tier · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Appsmith is an open-source platform for building admin panels, internal dashboards and operational apps on top of databases and APIs you already run. Licensed Apache 2.0 for the community edition, free to self-host, with paid tiers for SSO, audit logs and enterprise controls. Marketing operations teams reach for it when campaign data lives in five places and nobody wants to check five tabs: a single dashboard over the ad platforms' APIs, a lead-quality console next to the CRM, or a promotion approval panel wired to the warehouse. Queries are SQL or JS against your own connections, interfaces are drag-and-drop, and everything stays inside your infrastructure when self-hosted. The trade-offs: it is a developer-leaning tool, so someone comfortable with SQL and JS should own it; and as a general internal-tools platform it ships no marketing-specific templates, so the first useful app is on you. At 40,000-plus GitHub stars it is the largest project in the internal-tools class we cover. We have not run Appsmith in production; this assessment is based on the documented architecture and public materials.
