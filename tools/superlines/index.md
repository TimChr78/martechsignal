## Overview

Superlines is an AI Search Intelligence platform that measures and grows brand visibility across answer engines - ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Its stated differentiator is data collection from the real user-facing AI interfaces rather than API outputs, which the vendor argues can diverge from what customers see; the practical consequence for a marketing team is visibility data that matches the answers their buyers get. The platform tracks 10+ engines, exposes everything through a UI, an API and an MCP server, and pairs monitoring with an Action Center that turns visibility gaps into tasks. Pricing starts at €79/month for three engines and 50 tracked prompts on one brand, scales through Pro at €199 (150 prompts, 3 brands, exports, site audits) and Growth at €379 (300 prompts, 7 brands, API access), with a 7-day free trial and 12% off annual billing. The company is Helsinki-based with agency programs as a core go-to-market. For teams already running AI-visibility spreadsheets, the evaluation question is whether real-interface collection and the MCP access are worth the subscription over manual prompt panels; assess it as a GEO analytics layer rather than a content-creation tool.

## Best for

Brand and agency teams that need defensible AI Search visibility numbers across multiple engines, and teams that want GEO data readable by agents through MCP.

## Not for

Teams wanting a free tier (entry is €79/mo after a 7-day trial) and content teams looking for an AI writing tool - Superlines measures visibility, it does not generate content.

## Review notes

Researched from public documentation, and vendor materials. Not a hands-on test.

Researched from the vendor's live site and pricing pages (September 2026). Not a hands-on review. The pitch that matters for marketing ops is the collection method: Superlines samples the actual AI Search interfaces your customers use, on the argument that API-mode outputs differ from what users see. That claim is worth testing in a trial against your own prompt panel before committing budget.

The agency angle is structural, not cosmetic: multi-brand workspaces, competitor tracking on every plan including Starter, and an agency program. Prompts tracked scale 50/150/300 across Starter/Pro/Growth, history runs 1/2/6 months, and API access gates at Growth - so the data leaves the dashboard only once you are three tiers up.

The MCP server is the notable integration decision: an agent such as Claude Code can query your AI Search visibility data directly, which fits the pattern of GEO analytics becoming infrastructure that agents read rather than another dashboard a human checks.

## Verdict

A GEO analytics layer for the AI-search era: real-interface collection, MCP access and agency-grade multi-brand support, priced from €79 per month.
