OpenOutreach

Agentic AI email automation — describe your product and target market, AI finds the leads

Email Marketing · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

OpenOutreach is an open-source, self-hosted AI sales agent for B2B lead generation and email outreach. Built in Python and available on GitHub, it automates the entire top-of-funnel prospecting workflow: discovering leads from a licensed third-party B2B data provider, qualifying them using AI analysis of company profiles and job roles, and then running personalized agentic email outreach sequences from your own mailbox. Unlike commercial tools like Lemlist, Woodpecker, or Instantly that charge per seat or per mailbox, OpenOutreach is free to self-host. You provide your own OpenAI API key and email credentials, and the platform handles the rest. Each email is individually generated based on the prospect's company, role, and publicly available signals, moving beyond mail-merge personalization toward genuine one-to-one relevance. The platform is designed for technical teams comfortable with self-hosting and API management. It's not a SaaS with a polished UI; it's a Python application that runs on your infrastructure, giving you full control over data, sending behavior, and compliance. This makes it particularly interesting for European companies under GDPR constraints who need to keep prospect data on their own servers. OpenOutreach competes with the growing category of AI SDR tools (11x, Artisan, Lyx) but occupies the open-source, self-hosted niche where none of the commercial players operate.

AI Capabilities

  • AI-powered cold email generation
