Macro

Open source workspace with a self-updating, agent-driven CRM and shared AI team memory

CRM · Freemium · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Macro is an open source office suite that puts email, chat, docs, tasks, calls, and a CRM in one app. The bet is that AI agents are only as useful as the context they can see, and your team's context is scattered across seven SaaS tools. It was founded in 2020 by Jacob Beckerman, backed by Andreessen Horowitz (the site claims $30M+ raised), and open sourced under AGPL-3.0 in mid-2026. It's built in Rust and SolidJS, and the speed obsession shows. The repo picked up roughly 2,500 GitHub stars and over 5,000 commits in its first few months out in the open. The AI parts are more real than most. The CRM updates itself: contacts, companies, and deal notes get assembled from your email, chat, docs, and tasks, so nobody on your team enters data by hand. Ask 'what's the latest with Hartwell' before a call and you get a recap with receipts. Team-level agent memory is built nightly from workspace content, email triage splits your inbox into Signal and Noise, drafts come out in your voice, and agents can take a task, finish it, and report back. MCP support means outside agents can search the workspace too, including PDFs parsed out of email attachments. The marketing angle is specific: this is relationship and pipeline management. It does not send campaigns, run lifecycle email, or manage ads. Pricing is simple: free for personal use, $40 per seat per month for teams, every module on every plan. You pay for limits and team features, not feature gates. Self-hosting is free under AGPL if you want to run it yourself. $40 a seat costs more than a budget CRM, but it's cheaper than paying for Superhuman, Slack, Notion, and Linear separately, which is exactly the stack Macro wants to replace. There is no free team tier, so trial it as an individual first. The closest tool in this directory is Attio. Attio is a dedicated AI-native CRM with strong enrichment and agentic workflows, and it stops there. You bolt other tools around it. Macro is a whole workspace where the CRM is a byproduct of how your team already communicates. If you need a CRM to drop into an existing stack, Attio is the safer pick. If you want to collapse your tools into one and let the pipeline update itself from email context, Macro is the more interesting gamble. Skip it if your marketing team needs campaign automation, lifecycle email, or ad tooling. None of that lives here. The open source project is also young, so self-hosters should expect rough edges. And if your org runs on Salesforce integrations and long compliance checklists, give the ecosystem time to catch up.

AI Capabilities

  • Agent-driven CRM that updates itself from email, chat, docs, and tasks
  • Team-level agent memory built nightly from workspace content
  • AI email triage with Signal/Noise inbox splitting and auto-tagging
  • Email drafting in your voice
  • Agents that take tasks, close them, and report back
  • MCP interface exposing unified workspace search to agents
