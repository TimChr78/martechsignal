Postmark

Fast, reliable transactional email delivery with industry-leading speed and uptime

Email Marketing · Freemium

Overview

Postmark is a transactional email service built around one goal: getting time-sensitive emails into inboxes as fast as possible. Acquired by ActiveCampaign, it operates as a standalone service for application email like password resets, order confirmations, receipts, and notification digests. Speed is the point. Postmark's infrastructure delivers transactional messages in about one second, with detailed tracking available for 45 days. That contrasts with marketing-focused ESPs where delivery can take minutes and bounce processing is batched. Postmark separates transactional and broadcast sending into distinct message streams with independent reputation management, so a marketing campaign gone wrong cannot poison the deliverability of password reset emails. It offers SMTP relay and a well-documented REST API with SDKs for most major languages. Pricing starts at $15/month for 10,000 emails, scaling linearly with volume. Postmark competes with SendGrid, Mailgun, and Amazon SES in the transactional space, and competes on deliverability reputation and simplicity rather than feature breadth. It does not include marketing automation, list management, or campaign builders. Those are handled by its parent company ActiveCampaign or other marketing platforms. For developers who need a transactional email service that just works, Postmark's reliability and deliverability justify the higher per-email cost compared to SES or the free SendGrid tier.
