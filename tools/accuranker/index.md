## Overview

AccuRanker is a rank tracking platform from Aarhus, Denmark. It does one job: it tells you where your keywords stand, updated daily, in the locations and languages you track. The feature set is built around that job. Keyword research with search intent, tagging, advanced filtering, SERP analysis, forecasting, and reporting with exports. Search Console and Google Analytics data sit next to position data in the same views. Every current plan includes unlimited users. Plans are metered by keyword count. Professional starts at EUR 224/month for 2,000 keywords, with 3,000 and 5,000 keyword slots on the same tier. Expert runs EUR 764/month and covers 10,000 to 25,000 keywords. Anything above 25,000 keywords is quoted by sales on Enterprise. Annual billing takes 10% off, and a 14-day refund policy applies from purchase. The AI feature is AccuLLM. It tracks brand performance in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode: average rank across tracked prompts, total brand mentions, domain citations with the top cited pages, a sentiment score from 1 to 100, suggested prompts to track, and a competitor view across visibility, rank and sentiment. It also reports how often the LLMs run a web search for a tracked prompt, plus your share of total mentions and citations. Share numbers are the ones to watch, since AI answers give a fixed number of slots per response. Expert adds dynamic tagging, Tag Cloud, AI CTR, AI Search Volume and Looker Studio. Enterprise adds Google BigQuery, a write API, raw SERP HTML, unlimited domains and a dedicated CSM. There is also an AccuRanker MCP server for querying data from agents.

## Best for

Agencies and in-house teams that need daily rank data at keyword volumes they can name, and want LLM visibility tracked next to it under one unlimited-user account.

## Not for

Buyers who want keyword research, backlink data and site audits in the same subscription, or teams with large keyword lists that cannot justify EUR 764/month for 10,000 terms.

## Review notes

Researched from public documentation, the source repository, and vendor materials. Not a hands-on test.

Using AccuRanker starts with slot planning. Keywords are the billing unit, so the practical question is which terms earn one. Typical setups split between head terms, brand terms, and a competitor monitoring set, then use tagging and advanced filtering to keep client or market reports readable. Daily updates run in every supported country, and data import from other rank trackers shortens migration. A 14-day refund policy from purchase makes the first fortnight a real evaluation window rather than a sunk cost.

Reporting is where the tiers separate. Professional covers daily updates, keyword research, search intent, tagging, filtering, Search Console and Analytics data, and exports. Expert adds dynamic tagging, a Tag Cloud, AI CTR, AI Search Volume estimates, Looker Studio and an unlimited read API. Enterprise adds BigQuery export, an unlimited write API, raw SERP HTML, unlimited domains and a named CSM. For teams piping rank data into a warehouse, Enterprise is the tier that includes it.

AccuLLM works like a small rank tracker aimed at LLM answers. You pick prompts to track, with suggestions offered per brand and business area, then read average rank, brand mentions, domain citations, sentiment on a 1 to 100 scale, and competitor benchmarks across visibility, rank and sentiment. Two derived numbers do the heavy lifting. The web search rate shows how often the LLM searched the web before answering, which tells you whether the answer is influenceable at all. Your share of mentions and citations shows whether you are gaining ground on competitors inside a fixed number of answer slots. Coverage is ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode, and tracked history starts when tracking starts.

## Verdict

A focused rank tracker with a well-built LLM add-on, priced for teams that already know which keywords matter. Look elsewhere if you want a full SEO suite behind one login.
