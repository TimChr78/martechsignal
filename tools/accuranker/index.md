# AccuRanker | MartechSignal review

Daily keyword rank tracking with AccuLLM visibility data for ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews

- Last verified: 2026-09-25

AccuRanker is a rank tracking platform from Aarhus, Denmark. It does one job: it tells you where your keywords stand, updated daily, in the locations and languages you track. The feature set is built around that job. Keyword research with search intent, tagging, advanced filtering, SERP analysis, forecasting, and reporting with exports. Search Console and Google Analytics data sit next to position data in the same views. Every current plan includes unlimited users. Plans are metered by keyword count. Professional starts at EUR 224/month for 2,000 keywords, with 3,000 and 5,000 keyword slots on the same tier. Expert runs EUR 764/month and covers 10,000 to 25,000 keywords. Anything above 25,000 keywords is quoted by sales on Enterprise. Annual billing takes 10% off, and a 14-day refund policy applies from purchase. The AI feature is AccuLLM. It tracks brand performance in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and AI Mode: average rank across tracked prompts, total brand mentions, domain citations with the top cited pages, a sentiment score from 1 to 100, suggested prompts to track, and a competitor view across visibility, rank and sentiment. It also reports how often the LLMs run a web search for a tracked prompt, plus your share of total mentions and citations. Share numbers are the ones to watch, since AI answers give a fixed number of slots per response. Expert adds dynamic tagging, Tag Cloud, AI CTR, AI Search Volume and Looker Studio. Enterprise adds Google BigQuery, a write API, raw SERP HTML, unlimited domains and a dedicated CSM. There is also an AccuRanker MCP server for querying data from agents.
