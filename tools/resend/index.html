Resend Review

Developer-first email API with React Email support and AI-powered deliverability

Email Marketing · Freemium

Overview

Resend is a developer-first email API platform that treats email infrastructure as a modern developer tool rather than a legacy SMTP service. Founded around 2023 by engineers who were frustrated with existing email APIs, Resend puts developer experience first. Its main innovations are React Email integration (developers build email templates using React components with live preview), a clean, well-typed SDK, and a dashboard that actually shows what engineers need to debug delivery. Resend handles both transactional email (verification codes, invoices, alerts) and marketing email (newsletters, product updates) through the same API, using separate domains and IP pools to keep sender reputations independent. It runs on AWS infrastructure, and its deliverability tooling includes domain verification wizards, DMARC/SPF/DKIM guidance, and open/click tracking. The free plan includes 100 emails/day, and the Pro plan at $20/month covers 50,000 emails. Resend competes with SendGrid, Mailgun, and Postmark for transactional sending, but its real differentiator is the React-based template workflow that lets frontend engineers build, preview, and test emails in their existing development environment. That makes it popular in the Next.js, Vercel, and React ecosystems. For teams that want a full marketing platform with campaign builders and audience segmentation, Resend is too low-level. For sending email from code, it is one of the better developer experiences available.

Hands-on notes

Resend is the developer-first email API that arrived with modern React-era tooling: clean SDKs, React Email templates as code, webhooks that are a pleasure to wire. The developer experience is arguably the best in the category right now, and deliverability on transactional sends held up in our tests.

It is young: marketing features are thin, and some teams will want longer track records for mission-critical volume. Pricing is competitive at small-to-medium scale. For developer-led teams shipping product email in 2026, it is the first tool we reach for.

Verdict

The best developer experience in email right now; volume senders should verify enterprise fit.
