Make

Visual automation platform for building complex workflows with AI agents and apps

Workflow Automation · Freemium

Overview

Make sits between Zapier's simplicity and n8n's depth. Its visual scenario builder is genuinely better than Zapier's linear editor. You can branch, loop, and handle errors visually without touching code. Founded in 2012, headquartered in Prague, and acquired by Celonis in 2020, Make occupies the middle ground where most technical marketing teams land. The key structural difference from Zapier is Make's operations model: each module in a scenario is one operation, not one task. A module that processes 100 bundles counts as 1 operation, not 100. This makes Make dramatically cheaper than Zapier for data-heavy workflows (CRM syncs, contact enrichment) while still being more expensive than n8n for deep multi-step automation. Make's AI features (agents, workflow suggestions, data transformation) are a step behind n8n in capability but ahead of Zapier in cost efficiency. The AI agent node doesn't consume extra operations the way Zapier's AI steps consume extra tasks. For marketing teams running AI-assisted content pipelines or lead enrichment workflows, this is a meaningful difference. Make is best for teams that want visual complexity without coding, data-heavy integrations where operations-based pricing beats tasks, and mid-size marketing operations teams that have outgrown Zapier but don't need the full flexibility or maintenance burden of self-hosted n8n.
