# Make | MartechSignal review

Visual automation platform for building complex workflows with AI agents and apps

- Page: https://martechsignal.com/tools/make/
- Category: Workflow Automation
- Pricing: Freemium
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-09-07

Make, the platform formerly known as Integromat, sits between Zapier's simplicity and n8n's depth. Founded in Prague in 2012 and acquired by Celonis in 2020, it is where technical marketing teams land when a linear editor stops being enough: scenarios are drawn as a graph, so branching, looping, and error handling are visible rather than buried in configuration.

Billing changed in August 2026, when credits replaced operations as the unit of account. By default one operation equals one credit, and each module action in a scenario, such as adding a Google Sheets row or fetching Gmail data, consumes one credit. Router modules and the error handlers (Rollback, Break, Resume, Commit, Ignore) consume none, some features running on Make's AI Provider consume more, and Make warns at 75% and 90% usage with optional auto purchase of extra credit bundles. Unused credits expire at the end of the billing term.

Plans are Free ($0, 1,000 credits a month, 2 active scenarios, a 15 minute minimum interval, 5 MB files), Core at $9 a month, Pro at $16, and Teams at $29, each for 10,000 credits a month with a slider running from 10,000 up to 8M+ credits, and Enterprise quoted. Annual billing saves 15% or more. Paid tiers raise maximum execution to 40 minutes, lift file limits to 100 to 500 MB, allow 5 GB of data transfer per 10,000 credits, and extend log storage to 30 or 60 days. No step or module limit is published.

AI is a stated product line: Make AI Agents are available on all plans, alongside Maia by Make, the AI Toolkit, the Make MCP Server, and a Code App billed at 2 credits per second.

For data heavy marketing work such as CRM syncs and contact enrichment, where one module iterates over many rows, credits cost less than Zapier's per task metering. Teams that need self hosting or unlimited execution should look at n8n instead.
