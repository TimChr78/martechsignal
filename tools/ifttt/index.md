## Overview

IFTTT is the oldest name in this comparison and the one aimed at a different room. Its Applet model connects a trigger in one service to an action in another with no builder to learn, and the service catalogue reaches into consumer devices that business-focused platforms ignore entirely. Verified plans: the free tier runs two Applets with unlimited runs at standard speeds, Pro at $2.99/month billed annually lifts the cap to 20 Applets with multi-action steps and webhooks, and Pro+ at $8.99/month billed annually goes unlimited with AI services, query and filter code, and multiple accounts per service. The yearly billing is the price that matters; the monthly rates are markedly higher. For marketing teams, the honest read is that IFTTT is a connectivity tool, not an automation platform. There is no branching to design, no data transformation worth the name, and the run speeds on lower tiers are throttled by design. It earns its place at the edges: social listening triggers, device events, quick personal workflows where a full platform would be absurd.
