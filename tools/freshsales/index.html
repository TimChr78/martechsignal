Freshsales

AI-powered CRM with built-in phone, email, and chat for sales teams

CRM · Freemium

Overview

Freshsales is Freshworks' CRM, and it's the most credible mid-market alternative to HubSpot for teams that want built-in communication tools without the suite bloat. Part of the Freshworks ecosystem (Freshdesk, Freshchat, Freshmarketer), Freshsales anchors the sales side with AI that's genuinely useful rather than checkbox features. The standout is Freddy AI, which handles lead scoring, deal insights, and predictive contact scoring natively. It analyzes engagement patterns across email, phone, and chat to surface which deals are at risk and which leads are ready to convert, without requiring a data science team to configure scoring models. Built-in phone (with call recording and transcription), email sync, and a visual workflow builder mean a sales team can run entirely inside Freshsales without jumping between tools. Plans range from a free tier (basic CRM for small teams) to Growth ($15/user/mo), Pro ($47/user/mo), and Enterprise ($83/user/mo, billed annually). The free plan is genuinely usable, with contact management, built-in phone, email, and deal pipeline, making it a strong starting point for teams that will scale into paid tiers. The Freshworks ecosystem is the real multiplier: add Freshdesk for support, Freshchat for conversational engagement, and Freshmarketer for campaign automation, all sharing the same contact data. The tradeoff: Freshsales is neither the most modern (Attio wins on data model flexibility) nor the most enterprise (Salesforce owns that tier). It occupies the pragmatic middle, with good AI, good built-in comms, good pricing, and a good ecosystem. For a 50-200 person company that wants a sales-first CRM with room to grow into marketing and support without switching platforms, it's the safest bet below the HubSpot price point.
