Phrasee Review

AI-powered enterprise content optimization for email, push, and SMS marketing

AI Content & Copywriting · Enterprise

MartechSignal editorial review · updated 2026-08-28

Overview

Phrasee is an enterprise AI copywriting platform specialized in generating and optimizing short-form marketing language: email subject lines, push notification text, social media ad copy, SMS messages, and in-app messaging. Unlike general-purpose AI writers, Phrasee's natural language generation models are trained exclusively on marketing copy and incorporate brand voice controls directly into the generation pipeline. This means marketers define their brand's tone, vocabulary, and prohibited language once, and all generated copy conforms to those rules without manual review. The platform includes A/B testing capabilities that measure real-world performance and feed the results back into the model so it generates increasingly effective copy over time. Analytics dashboards track lift in open rates, click-through rates, and conversion against brand-specific benchmarks. Phrasee was founded in the UK and acquired by CM Group (now Marigold) in 2021, then underwent a management buyout in 2023 to operate independently. It is enterprise-only with custom pricing based on message volume and channels. Primary competitors are Persado (emotional intelligence approach) and the AI copy features in Braze, Salesforce, and Adobe platforms. Phrasee's key pitch is brand safety at scale: for organizations where sending off-brand AI-generated copy to millions of customers is a career-limiting risk, Phrasee provides guardrails that consumer AI tools do not.

Hands-on notes

Phrasee pioneered AI subject-line and push-copy generation, focusing narrowly on email and mobile messaging language. Its brand-voice controls are the differentiator: generated lines stay inside trained tone boundaries, which addresses the biggest fear brands have about AI copy.

Like Persado, it is an enterprise product with enterprise onboarding, and its best results come with high send volumes where percentage lifts are meaningful. Teams sending a few campaigns monthly should invest in copywriters and testing discipline instead.

Verdict

Focused, brand-safe AI copy for high-volume email programs; wrong fit below enterprise volume.
