Overview

Hightouch is the leading warehouse-native activation layer, commonly filed under composable CDP: instead of collecting user events itself, it queries the data warehouse your company already runs (Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift) and syncs the results as audiences, traits, and events into marketing, sales, and advertising destinations such as Salesforce, Braze, Klaviyo, Meta Ads, and Google Ads. Nothing is stored natively - the warehouse stays the source of truth, which appeals to teams that already invested in a modern data stack and do not want a second duplicate profile store. Identity resolution, match booster, and real-time personalization extend it beyond batch reverse ETL, and AI Decisioning optimizes campaign variants with reinforcement learning. Self-serve pricing counts active syncs (2 on the free plan, 10 on self-serve) with no seat or destination caps; business tiers are quoted. Acquired positioning has shifted rapidly - reverse ETL (2022), composable CDP (2023), agentic marketing platform (2025-2026) - under competitive pressure from CDP incumbents and the AI-personalization wave. This assessment is based on vendor documentation and public materials.

Pricing

Hightouch is freemium, with a free tier to start.

Free plan: 2 active syncs/mo, hourly frequency, no destination or seat limits. Self-serve tier: 10 active syncs/mo. Business tier on usage+seats (vendor docs publish no number for it; third-party estimates put entry deployments around $1,000+/mo - unverified, treat as indicative only). Operations cap 100M/mo. (Docs checked 2026-10-01.)
