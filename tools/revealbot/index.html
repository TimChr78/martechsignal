Revealbot (Birch)

AI-powered ad automation and rules engine for Meta, Google, and TikTok ads

Advertising & Paid Media · From $99/mo

Overview

Revealbot, rebranded as Birch (bir.ch), is a rules-based advertising automation platform built primarily for Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads, with growing support for Google Ads and TikTok. Unlike AI-driven platforms that use machine learning to optimize campaigns on their own (like Albert AI or Smartly.io), Birch uses deterministic if/then rules. Marketers define explicit conditions and actions ("if ROAS drops below 1.5, decrease budget by 20%"; "if CTR exceeds 3%, increase budget by 30%"), and Birch executes them in real time. This rules-based approach gives performance marketers precise, predictable control over campaign automation without the black-box uncertainty of AI optimization. Key features include a rule builder (a visual interface for creating automation rules with conditions on any available metric, such as ROAS, CPA, CTR, CPM, frequency, and reach, and actions on budgets, bids, ad status, and campaign settings), bulk ad management (create, duplicate, edit, and manage thousands of ads across hundreds of ad sets with spreadsheet-like efficiency, which matters for DTC brands running hyper-segmented campaigns), automated reporting (scheduled reports to Slack, email, or Google Sheets with customizable metrics and formatting), ad performance alerts (real-time notifications when key metrics cross thresholds), split testing automation (automatically declare winners in A/B tests based on statistical significance and scale winning variations), and cross-platform management (Meta as primary, with Google Ads and TikTok support in development). Birch's pricing starts at about $99/month for basic rules and scales with ad spend volume and rule complexity, typically $200-1,000+/month for agencies and DTC brands. It competes with Madgicx (AI-powered Meta ad optimization, less rules-based), AdEspresso (Hootsuite-owned, simpler A/B testing), and Revealbot's own pre-rebrand positioning (essentially the same tool with a new name and expanded scope). Birch suits performance marketers and DTC brands running Meta-heavy ad strategies who want precise control over automation, the type of marketer who would rather write 50 explicit automation rules than trust an AI to make decisions. It pairs well with AI creative tools (AdCreative.ai, Pencil) in a stack: Birch handles the media buying rules while AI tools handle the creative. Its main limitation is the Meta-centric focus. If your ad spend is diversified across TikTok, YouTube, and programmatic, Birch covers only a portion of your stack.
