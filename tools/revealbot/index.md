# Revealbot (Birch) | MartechSignal review

AI-powered ad automation and rules engine for Meta, Google, and TikTok ads

- Page: https://martechsignal.com/tools/revealbot/
- Category: Advertising & Paid Media
- Pricing: From $49/mo
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-09-07

Revealbot is now Bïrch (bir.ch), and the rebrand came with a product expansion worth knowing before you compare it to anything. The core is still condition-based automation for paid social: rules that watch Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat ad accounts and act on budgets, bids, statuses, and custom metrics, with the company stating it is an official partner of those platforms. Around that core sit newer modules. Explorer tracks ad performance, flags creative fatigue, and ranks assets. Stage builds ads by duplicating your setups from Google Drive media. Hub adds server-side tracking for Meta through Signals Gateway, priced per event from free at 10,000 monthly events to $499 at 150 million. Launcher generates ad sets from bulk creative uploads. Bïrch AI is described as an AI layer across your workflows, and Bïrch MCP connects the platform to external AI tools; Routines for scheduling repeated workflows are listed as coming soon. The integration list is attribution-heavy: Slack, Google Analytics, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Adjust, Hyros, Wicked Reports, and AppsFlyer, mostly so their metrics can drive rule conditions and custom metrics. Pricing is tiered by monthly ad spend across connected accounts, with Essential at $49 per month covering workspaces, post boosting, reports, and Slack alerts, and Pro at $99 adding automated rules and strategies, Explorer, Launcher, Stage, and custom and lookalike audiences; Enterprise is quoted with no limits or overages. Annual billing works out to twelve months for the price of ten, and there is a 14-day free trial with no credit card. The company claims 15,000+ clients, $2B in annual ad spend managed, and 240 million automated actions per year. What it is not is an autonomous optimizer: you write the conditions and Bïrch executes them, which is exactly the point for media buyers who want predictable automation.
