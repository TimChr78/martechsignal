Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, HubSpot CRM brings sales, service, and marketing workflows into one platform around a unified contact record. It supports pipeline management, deal tracking, email logging, meeting scheduling, ticketing, and campaign reporting, with native connections to Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Zapier, Salesforce, Shopify, Stripe, and Google Analytics. The platform is a fit for startups, small businesses, and mid-market teams that need an accessible system of record without managing separate tools for outreach, support, and analytics. An open API allows custom integrations, although the product itself is not open source.

HubSpot's main differentiator is its combination of a free CRM forever plan with optional paid hubs that scale as teams grow. Sales Hub Starter starts at $20 per seat per month, Professional at $100, and Enterprise at $150, with higher tiers adding automation, permissions, and reporting depth. Its AI capabilities include an AI email writer, predictive lead scoring, AI call transcription, AI meeting scheduler, and AI content suggestions, which help reduce manual work across sales and service conversations. Compared with alternatives such as Salesforce, Pipedrive, or Zoho CRM, HubSpot tends to emphasize ease of use and cross-team alignment more than deep enterprise customization. It is best for growing businesses that want a free-to-start CRM with practical AI features and room to expand into marketing and service tools.
