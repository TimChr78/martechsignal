Overview

Activepieces is an open-source automation platform that treats self-hosting as a first-class option rather than an enterprise footnote. The same product runs on their cloud, on your own servers, or fully air-gapped, and the pricing page says so in plain words instead of hiding it behind a sales call. The commercial model is built on credits with flat monthly prices: Plus at $20/month buys 10,000 credits and up to five users with no per-task staircase, and overage runs $0.007 per credit. Teams that outgrow it move to the $200/month Team tier for 50,000 credits, 25 users, and SSO. The free tier is real rather than a trial: 100 credits a day, unlimited flows, no card required. The AI story is the interesting part for agent-curious teams. Flows, agents, and tables live in one builder, MCP and API access ship on the free tier, and the paid tiers let you bring your own model keys instead of paying a markup on tokens. For a team that wants automation infrastructure it can actually inspect and move, this is one of the few honest options in the category.
