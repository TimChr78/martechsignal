Overview

Semrush is the closest thing the SEO industry has to an operating system: a platform that spans keyword research, competitive analysis, rank tracking, site auditing, content optimization, link building, paid advertising intelligence, social media management, and increasingly, AI search visibility. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Boston, Semrush serves over 10 million users from freelancers to enterprise marketing teams. Its keyword database covers 25 billion keywords across 140+ countries, and its backlink index processes trillions of URLs. The platform's breadth is its defining trait: a single subscription replaces a half-dozen point tools for most SEO workflows. Key capabilities include the Domain Overview (competitive benchmarking at a glance), Keyword Magic Tool (keyword discovery with advanced filtering), Position Tracking (daily rank monitoring), Site Audit (technical SEO crawler with 140+ checks), and the Content Marketing Toolkit (topic research, content templates, and SEO writing assistant). Semrush has been investing in AI features including an AI writing assistant, automated content brief generation, and Copilot conversational search. Pricing starts at $117/month billed annually ($140 monthly) for the Pro plan with 5 projects; the Guru plan at $250/month adds content marketing features and historical data; the Business plan at $500/month includes API access and white-label reporting. The main criticisms are cost (it is the most expensive all-in-one SEO platform), complexity (most users only touch 20-30% of features), and data accuracy on smaller or niche markets where Ahrefs' crawl data is sometimes more current. For teams that need one platform for SEO research, content, and competitive intel, Semrush is the default choice.
