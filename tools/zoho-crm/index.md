## Overview

Zoho CRM covers pipeline automation, lead scoring and forecasting with its Zia assistant. The free tier takes 3 users and the paid editions attach the wider Zoho suite (mail, books, desk) at low per-seat prices.

## AI Capabilities

- Zia assistant for lead scoring, deal prediction and email sentiment
- AI forecasting and anomaly alerts on pipeline data
- Conversational AI assistant for querying CRM data in plain language
## Key Integrations

- Zoho Mail
- Zoho Books
- Zoho Desk
- Google Workspace
- Microsoft 365
- Zapier
## Pricing

Zoho CRM is freemium, with a free tier to start, paid plans start at €14/mo as of 2026-09.

Free for 3 users; Standard EUR 14/user/mo; Professional EUR 23/user/mo (automation and AI); Enterprise EUR 40/user/mo; Ultimate EUR 52/user/mo (EU page, ex. VAT)

Current plans and limits live on the [Zoho CRM pricing page](https://www.zoho.com/crm/pricing.html).

## Best for

Tiny teams that want a real CRM free: the free tier covers 3 users with pipeline automation, lead scoring basics, and email included. EU buyers who prefer euro-denominated per-seat pricing scale from Standard at EUR 14 to Professional at EUR 23 (automation and AI) and Enterprise at EUR 40.

## Not for

Buyers who want a scored assessment against our rubric: Zoho CRM is one of three catalog tools not yet scored (see the methodology page). Teams that need a self-hostable codebase should look at the open-source CRM options instead; Zoho is proprietary.
