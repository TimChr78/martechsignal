Loops

Modern email marketing platform for SaaS with AI-powered campaigns and automation

Email Marketing · Freemium

Overview

Loops is a modern email platform purpose-built for SaaS companies that want lightweight, developer-friendly email tooling without the complexity of enterprise platforms. Launched around 2022, Loops combines marketing email, lifecycle automation, and transactional sending in a single product with a deliberately simple interface. Where Mailchimp tries to cover everything and Customer.io serves complex lifecycle operations, Loops focuses on doing one category of work well. The platform handles onboarding sequences, feature announcement campaigns, re-engagement drips, and transactional messages like password resets from the same dashboard. Templates are clean and modern; the editor prioritizes speed over endless customization. Event tracking via API or Segment powers behavioral triggers, and the segmentation builder supports common SaaS use cases like filtering by plan tier, feature usage, or trial status. Loops offers a generous free tier and paid plans that scale with contact count, making it accessible for early-stage startups. It competes most directly with Customer.io and Userlist in the SaaS lifecycle email space, differentiating on simplicity and setup speed. The trade-off is depth: Loops does not support SMS, push notifications, or in-app messaging, so teams needing multi-channel automation will outgrow it or pair it with other tools.
