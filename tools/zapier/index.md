# Zapier | MartechSignal review

No-code automation platform connecting 9,000+ apps with AI-powered workflows

- Page: https://martechsignal.com/tools/zapier/
- Category: Workflow Automation
- Pricing: Freemium
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-09-07

Zapier is the automation platform most people mean when they say they want to connect two tools without writing code. It lists more than 9,000 app integrations, still the widest catalogue in this directory, and the niche martech tools that lack an n8n node or a Make module usually still have a Zapier integration. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in San Francisco, it is the default choice for marketing teams moving form submissions into a CRM, pushing new leads to Slack, or registering event attendees into an email sequence.

Pricing is task based, and the definition matters: a task is counted when a Zap successfully moves data or completes an action, triggers do not count, and failed actions do not count. What does count is every step in a Zap and every external connector call, so a twenty step workflow consumes roughly twenty tasks per run. The Free plan covers 100 tasks a month and two step Zaps only. Professional starts at $19.99 per month billed annually at the 750 task tier, or $29.99 monthly, scaling to 2M tasks. Team starts at $69 per month annually with 2,000 tasks, 25 seats, and shared workspaces. Premium apps are excluded from Free, and polling intervals run 15 minutes on Free, 2 minutes on Professional, and 1 minute on Team and up.

AI is now a product line rather than a feature. Zapier Agents, Chatbots, Canvas, Zapier MCP, and Copilot are listed on every plan with basic access, AI by Zapier is not available on Free, and Agents are billed separately in activities rather than tasks, with 400 activities a month free and 1,500 on Agents Pro.

Buy Zapier for breadth and onboarding speed, and move to Make or n8n when branching logic or volume makes the per task price hurt.
