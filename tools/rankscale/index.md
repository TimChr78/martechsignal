# Rankscale | MartechSignal review

AI visibility tracking across 17+ answer engines for agencies and enterprise teams

- Page: https://martechsignal.com/tools/rankscale/
- Category: GEO & LLM Optimization
- Pricing: From $99/mo
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-09-25

Rankscale is a Vienna-built AI visibility platform for agencies and enterprise teams. It tracks brand mentions, rankings, citations and sentiment across 17-plus answer engines, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, DeepSeek, Grok, Copilot and Mistral, with no per-engine upsell. The feature set covers competitor analysis, sources box inspection, shopping and ad tracking in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Copilot, query fan-out (the internal searches engines run before answering), page audits with AI-readiness scoring, and prompt research that estimates demand through semantic reconstruction.

Monitoring runs on credits. Each query to an AI engine costs a fraction of a credit, typically 0.25. Pro starts at EUR 99 per month with 1,200 credits. Pro is EUR 99 with 1,200 credits (up to 4,800 tracked answers), 10 brand dashboards and 50 page audits, and a seven-day trial. Growth is EUR 385 with 5,500 credits, 50 dashboards, 200 audits, white-label options, a REST API and agency benefits. Enterprise is EUR 780 with 12,000 credits and 100 dashboards. Yearly billing saves 15 percent, and unused credits roll over to a cap of two to three times the monthly allocation.

Rankscale GmbH says 1,000-plus active users and lists Bosch, UBS and Cartier on its customer wall. GA4 and Search Console connectors are beta.

Two honest caveats: credits make monthly cost harder to predict than fixed prompt caps, and features like Scout recommendations and the GA4 tie-in are marked beta. For agencies that want one price for every engine and Looker Studio exports, the credit model is the main thing to size before committing.
