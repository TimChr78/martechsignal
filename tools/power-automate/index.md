# Microsoft Power Automate | MartechSignal review

Enterprise workflow automation inside the Microsoft Power Platform

- Page: https://martechsignal.com/tools/power-automate/
- Category: Workflow Automation
- Pricing: From $15/mo
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-09-27

Power Automate is the workflow layer of Microsoft's Power Platform, and its real competitor is not Zapier but the question of whether your organization already lives in Microsoft 365. Where it wins, it wins on proximity: connectors to SharePoint, Dataverse, and the Office suite that no third party can match, plus the governance surface that enterprise IT departments already know how to audit.

Verified pricing on the vendor's page: Premium at $15 per user per month paid yearly covers cloud flows and attended RPA. Unattended robotic process automation is priced per bot at $150/month, and Microsoft-hosted bots at $215/month. The per-bot pricing is where teams get surprised, so capacity planning matters more here than in task-priced competitors.

The honest caveat is the platform gravity. Flows, connections, and custom connectors accumulate inside the tenant, the licensing model rewards staying, and building outside the Microsoft estate means paying premium prices for convenience you may not need. Inside that estate, though, it is the shortest path between an event in Outlook and an action in the rest of your stack.
