Dynamic Yield Review

AI-powered personalization platform for web, mobile, and email experiences

Personalization & CDP · Enterprise

Overview

Dynamic Yield by Mastercard is an enterprise AI personalization platform built around Experience OS, a real-time decisioning engine that algorithmically matches content, products, and offers to each individual customer across web, mobile, email, and push notifications. Acquired by Mastercard in 2022, Dynamic Yield holds the longest-running leadership streak in personalization: **8 consecutive Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader recognitions** (most recently 2026), making it the most decorated vendor in the category. The platform's core capabilities span **website personalization** (A/B testing, dynamic content, product recommendations), **email and push notification personalization** (trigger-based, behavior-driven), **audience management** (real-time segmentation with predictive affinity scoring), and **AI-driven optimization** that automatically discovers the most effective targeting setup for each campaign with a single click. A standout feature is the **Executive Dashboard** which translates complex personalization performance data into C-suite-ready reports, addressing the common gap between personalization execution and business reporting. Dynamic Yield is used by McDonald's (menu personalization across drive-thru and kiosks), IKEA, Sephora, and other global retailers. It integrates with major ecommerce platforms (Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Adobe Commerce, SAP), CDPs, and analytics tools. As a Mastercard company, it benefits from Mastercard's data ecosystem and global infrastructure. Pricing is enterprise-tier, typically starting in the mid-five-figures annually depending on traffic volume and feature scope. The platform is best suited for large retail and ecommerce organizations that need a proven personalization engine with deep experimentation capabilities and executive-level reporting.

Best for

Enterprise ecommerce and media teams running personalization at scale, especially those already in the Mastercard ecosystem who want testing, recommendations, and journey orchestration from one vendor.

Not for

Small teams without dedicated optimization staff. Enterprise contracts, annual commitments, and implementation effort make it a poor fit below roughly seven-figure online revenue.

Hands-on notes

Dynamic Yield, now under Mastercard, is the most decorated personalization vendor in the category: eight consecutive Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader placements, most recently 2026. Its Experience OS makes real-time decisions that match content, products, and offers to each visitor across web, mobile, email, and push, with A/B testing and recommendations layered in. The commerce heritage shows in how natively it handles product feeds and merchandising.

Everything about it assumes scale. Contracts are enterprise, implementation runs through services, and the platform needs real traffic and a dedicated team before the personalization math pays off. Mastercard's ownership direction is still settling, so roadmap questions linger. Mid-market teams end up paying for capability they cannot staff, which is the classic enterprise-tool trap. Demo it against your real traffic before any contract talk.

Verdict

The safest enterprise bet in personalization, with the analyst streak to prove it. Too heavy for mid-market.

What are Dynamic Yield triggers?

Triggers are rules that fire experiences in real time: exit intent, scroll depth, cart abandonment, weather, referrer, or any first-party or third-party signal. They power pop-ups, recommendation refreshes, and message swaps inside Experience OS without code changes.
