AdCreative.ai

AI platform generating high-converting ad creatives and social media post designs

Advertising & Paid Media · From $39/mo

Overview

AdCreative.ai is an AI-powered ad creative generation platform that produces high-converting ad creatives — images, videos, and copy — at scale. Its core value proposition is simple: upload your brand assets and logo, define your target audience, and the platform generates dozens of ready-to-deploy ad variations with built-in performance prediction scoring. For teams whose creative bottleneck is producing enough variations to test across platforms, AdCreative.ai turns a 2-week designer-and-copywriter cycle into a 10-minute AI workflow. Key features include: **AI Creative Generator** (produces static image ads, video ads, carousels, and social media post creatives from brand guidelines, product images, and audience targeting — with automatic sizing for every platform's specifications), **Creative Insights Pro** (a performance prediction model that scores each generated creative with an expected CTR/conversion rate before it runs — helping prioritize which variations to test first), **AI Copywriting** (generates headlines, ad copy, and CTAs optimized for each platform — Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest), **Brand Kit** (upload brand colors, fonts, logo, and style guidelines to ensure all generated creatives stay on-brand), **Competitor Analysis** (analyze competitor ad creatives and generate improved variations), and **Team Collaboration** (shared asset libraries, approval workflows, and creative versioning). AdCreative.ai integrates directly with Facebook Ads, Google Ads, and LinkedIn Ads for one-click creative deployment. Pricing is credit-based: Starter from $39/month (10 credits/mo), Professional at $69/month (25 credits), Ultimate at $149/month (100 credits), and Enterprise custom. The platform competes with Canva (broader design, less AI ad specialization), Pencil AI (video-first, brand safety focus), and Predis.ai (social media focus with scheduling). AdCreative.ai is best suited for performance marketing teams at mid-market DTC and ecommerce brands where the primary constraint is creative volume — running dozens of ad variations per platform requires a creative production pipeline that manual design can't sustain.
