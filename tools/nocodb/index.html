NocoDB Review

Free, self-hostable Airtable alternative that turns any database into a smart spreadsheet

Marketing Automation · Free tier · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

NocoDB turns any SQL database into a smart, Airtable-style spreadsheet with forms, views, permissions and automations on top. It is free to self-host under a Sustainable Use License (fair-code: free to use, not free to resell as a service; the same class as n8n's license), with paid cloud and enterprise tiers. For marketing teams it is the most direct self-hosted answer to the spreadsheet sprawl that runs campaign ops: content calendars, influencer and partner trackers, budgets, launch checklists and lead lists become shared, permissioned bases over your own Postgres or MySQL, with forms feeding them and webhooks pushing events into the rest of the stack. Compared with Airtable, you give up polish and the native integrations catalog and gain data ownership, no per-seat cost and SQL access to everything. Compared with NocoBase or Budibase, it is much faster to value: if a structured table with views and forms covers the need, NocoDB is the shortest path, and it is the most popular project in this class at 64,000-plus GitHub stars. We have not run NocoDB in production; this assessment is based on the documented architecture and public materials.
