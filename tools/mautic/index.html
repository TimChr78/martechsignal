Mautic

Open-source marketing automation platform with email, campaigns, and lead management

Open-Source Tools · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina, USA, Mautic is an open-source marketing automation platform designed to manage campaigns, email marketing, and lead lifecycle processes without relying on proprietary SaaS infrastructure. It supports contact management, landing pages, forms, segmentation, automated workflows, and multi-channel campaign orchestration, giving teams direct control over their data and deployment environment. The project is released under the GPL license and has attracted more than 10,000 GitHub stars, reflecting an active community of contributors and users. Mautic is suited to marketing teams, agencies, developers, and organizations that want flexibility and ownership rather than a managed black-box tool. Its API and integration ecosystem connect it with Salesforce, HubSpot, WordPress, Zapier, Slack, Google Analytics, and Stripe, allowing it to fit into existing CRM, analytics, and commerce stacks. AI-related capabilities include AI-powered lead scoring, AI email personalization, and AI campaign optimization, which can help teams prioritize prospects, tailor messaging, and refine performance over time. Compared with commercial alternatives such as HubSpot Marketing Hub or Marketo, Mautic trades polished hosted convenience for self-hosted control, lower licensing costs, and deeper technical customization. Pricing is free and open source for the core software, while paid hosting, managed services, and professional support are available from third-party providers. This model makes it attractive for teams with technical resources that can maintain infrastructure, security, and upgrades internally. Best for technically capable marketing operations teams, agencies, and privacy-conscious organizations seeking a customizable, self-hosted alternative to commercial marketing automation platforms.
