Frase

AI-powered SEO content platform for research, writing, and AI visibility tracking

SEO & Search · From $15/mo

Overview

Frase is an AI content optimization platform that positions itself as a content operating system for the era of AI search. Where Clearscope and Surfer SEO focus on Google rankings, Frase expands the scope to include AI search visibility (Answer Engine Optimization) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). Its core workflow mirrors the category: research a topic by analyzing top SERP results, build a content brief with target terms and questions, then write and optimize using a content editor that scores topic coverage in real time. Frase differentiates through its AI search features: SERP analysis for AI-generated results, question research that surfaces the exact queries appearing in Google's People Also Ask and featured snippets, and AI answer generation that produces content structured for AI overview citations. The platform includes an AI writer that generates full drafts, an SEO content brief tool, and a WordPress integration for direct publishing. Pricing starts at $15/month for the Solo plan with one user and 10 document credits; the Basic plan at $45/month adds 30 credits and SERP analysis; the Team plan at $115/month includes unlimited documents and 3 user seats. Frase competes with Clearscope and Surfer on content optimization and Jasper on AI writing, while its AI search visibility angle is a unique wedge. The trade-off: its content editor, while functional, is less polished than Clearscope's, and its AI-generated content still requires human editing for publication quality.

Hands-on notes

Frase started as a brief-and-write SEO tool and now leans into AI drafting. The core loop: give it a topic, it assembles SERP research into an outline, and the editor generates sections from your sources. The quality of generated copy is usable for foundations but needs a human pass, which the workflow assumes anyway.

Pricing is mid-tier with an annual discount. It competes with Surfer and Clearscope on briefs, and with Jasper on drafting. The differentiator is the combined brief-to-draft loop in one interface.

Verdict

Efficient for teams that want research and first-draft in one place, then human polish on top.
