Nosto

AI-powered ecommerce personalization with product recommendations and merchandising

Personalization & CDP · Enterprise

Overview

Nosto is an AI-powered Commerce Experience Platform (CXP) that delivers ecommerce personalization through a unified AI engine powering three integrated modules: product recommendations, personalized search and discovery, and category merchandising. Unlike platforms that treat search, recommendations, and merchandising as separate products bolted together, Nosto's single AI brain shares behavioral data across all three. What a shopper clicks in search results directly informs the recommendations they see on product pages and the sort order of category listings. The platform's key capabilities include behavioral product recommendations (homepage, PDP, cart, checkout, post-purchase), personalized site search that learns from click-through and purchase patterns, visual merchandising with drag-and-drop rule configuration for category pages, dynamic segmentation based on real-time behavior and purchase history, and A/B testing and analytics built into every personalization module. Nosto's search product, strengthened by two dedicated search acquisitions, offers typo-tolerant, AI-ranked results with individual result boosting and synonym management. Nosto's pricing is GMV-based (gross merchandise value) rather than query-based or traffic-based. Merchants pay a fixed percentage of revenue through the platform, which removes per-query cost anxiety for high-traffic stores. The platform integrates with Shopify, Magento (Adobe Commerce), BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and custom storefronts via API. Nosto competes primarily with Clerk.io, Dynamic Yield, and Algolia in the commerce personalization space. It positions as the unified alternative where businesses don't need to stitch together separate search, recommendation, and merchandising tools. Best suited for mid-market to enterprise ecommerce brands that want one AI platform covering the full on-site personalization stack.
