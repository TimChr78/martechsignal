## Overview

Pencil is a generative-AI creative platform that has grown from ad generation into what it now calls the AI operating system for marketing, operating under Pencil AI Limited with Brandtech Group branding still across the site. The product claim is orchestration: it aggregates AI models from OpenAI, Google, Adobe, Runway, and Bria in one layer, selects the right model per task, and wraps them in enterprise controls - a no-train policy, IP indemnification, regional data compliance across EU, US, and APAC, role-based access, brand safety guardrails, and SOC 2 Type II certification. Generation covers text, images, video, and ad creative, and the pricing page adds launch-and-track advertising natively on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google Display, DV360, and LinkedIn. Integrations follow the enterprise pattern rather than a marketplace: ad platforms (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, DV360, CM360), DAM systems (Bynder and Orange Logic generally available, Adobe Workfront in closed beta, AEM coming soon), Monotype for fonts, and GWI for consumer research, with an Insights Agent built on that partnership. Pencil&#x27;s own case studies name Experian, Diageo, L&#x27;Oreal, Barilla, and Japan Airlines. Pricing is now self-serve rather than enterprise-only: Core at $14 per month ($11 billed annually) with 50 generations, Growth at $55 ($44 annual) with 250 generations and unlimited workspaces, and Pro at custom pricing with unlimited generations, feed-based bulk generation, and the full governance terms. Generations are the metering unit, so creative volume rather than seats drives the bill. The honest caveats: the site leads with vendor-supplied outcome claims (50 percent lower production costs, 79 percent ROAS improvement), and the plan comparison table still marks several capabilities as coming soon, so confirm what is live before you commit. It suits brands shipping high ad-variant volumes under brand governance; a solo team running a few campaigns a quarter has cheaper options.

## How to install

- Start from the pricing page. Core and Growth have self-serve Get Started flows, so you can create an account and generate without talking to sales.
- Book a demo if you want Pro: unlimited generations, feed-based bulk generation across audiences, markets, and products, and the enterprise terms are all demo-gated.
- Connect the ad platforms you run - Meta, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, YouTube, DV360, CM360 - and a DAM such as Bynder if your assets live there.
- Set the brand library and guardrails before generating. Governance (no-train, brand safety, role-based access) is positioned as the default rather than an option, and Pro adds IP assignment and data ringfencing to the terms.
- Plan the bill in generations: 50 per month on Core, 250 on Growth, and committed consumption across models and modalities on Pro. Enterprise add-ons cover managed integrations with your stack, managed fine-tuning, and bring-your-own models.
## Best for

Brand and agency teams producing high ad-variant volumes across many markets that need enterprise governance around generative creative: no-train terms, IP indemnification, brand guardrails, and regional data compliance.

## Not for

Teams that want a cheap generate-and-go tool with a large template library, and anyone who needs a public API or a broad third-party marketplace. The documented integration surface is ad platforms, DAMs, Monotype, and GWI.

## Review notes

Assessed from trypencil.com rather than an account. The repositioning is recent and visible in the copy: the AI operating system for marketing, model aggregation across OpenAI, Google, Adobe, Runway, and Bria, and governance language aimed at procurement and legal teams - no-train policy, indemnification, data ringfencing, SOC 2 Type II.

Pricing changed shape and is now self-serve at the bottom. Core and Growth are metered in generations (50 and 250 per month), Pro is custom-priced with unlimited generations, committed consumption, feed-based bulk generation, and the enterprise terms. Volume, not seats, is what you budget.

The integration list is ad-platform and DAM-centric rather than a marketplace: Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, DV360, and CM360 for launching and trafficking; Bynder, Orange Logic, Workfront, and AEM for assets; GWI for research data feeding the Insights Agent. Several plan features are labeled coming soon on the comparison table, so confirm at signup.

## Verdict

Generative creative with enterprise governance on top, now priced self-serve at the low end. Confirm which features are live and budget in generations rather than seats.
