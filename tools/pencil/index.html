Pencil

AI-powered ad creative generation and performance prediction for paid media

Advertising & Paid Media · From $39/mo

Overview

Pencil is an AI-powered creative platform that has evolved from a performance prediction engine into a full creative generation and governance platform. Originally known as "Pencil by The Brandtech Group," the platform now positions itself as an AI operating system for marketing, aggregating multiple AI models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, and specialized creative models), enforcing enterprise brand governance, and translating production efficiency gains into increased media investment. Unlike tools that only generate ads, Pencil's philosophy is to save on production and reinvest in media. The efficiency of AI creative production should directly fuel more advertising reach. The platform's capabilities include AI creative generation (video ads, static display ads, social media content, and animated HTML5 banners, generated from product feeds, brand guidelines, and existing assets, with platform-specific sizing). Performance prediction, Pencil's heritage feature, uses a machine learning model that predicts how each creative will perform on each platform before it runs, scoring on engagement, conversion probability, and brand safety. Brand safety and governance provides enterprise-grade controls for AI-generated content: brand guideline enforcement, automated content moderation, human-in-the-loop approval workflows, and compliance with platform advertising policies. Multi-model aggregation routes creative generation tasks to the optimal AI model (video generation to specialized video models, copy to GPT/Claude, image variants to DALL-E/Midjourney) rather than using a single model for everything. Creative analytics provides post-launch performance tracking with insights that feed back into the prediction model for continuous improvement. Pencil is built for enterprise brands and agencies managing high-volume creative production across multiple markets and platforms. Pricing is enterprise-tier (custom, typically $10K-50K+/year depending on creative volume and seats). It competes with AdCreative.ai (template-based, SMB-friendly, no governance), Smartly.io (creative automation + media management, larger platform), and Celtra (creative production platform, less AI automation). Pencil is best suited for global brands and holding company agencies where the creative production pipeline is a genuine bottleneck, producing 500+ ad variations per campaign across 20 markets with brand governance requirements that make pure AI-generation tools too risky. Its emphasis on governance and brand safety differentiates it from generate-and-go tools. It is the platform you choose when you need AI speed with enterprise control.
