# Hootsuite | MartechSignal review

Social media management platform with AI-powered scheduling and analytics

- Category: Social Media
- Pricing: From $99/mo
- Open source: no
- Last verified: 2026-08-28

Hootsuite is one of the oldest social media management platforms, and it has grown from a simple multi-account scheduler into a full social media management suite. In 2026, it offers the broadest AI feature set for teams managing multiple platforms at scale, combining scheduling, content creation, analytics, social listening, employee advocacy, and social commerce across 20+ social networks from a single dashboard.

The platform covers a wide range of functions. Publishing and scheduling includes bulk scheduling, a content calendar, AI-optimized posting times, and a content library for reusable assets. AI content creation runs through OwlyWriter AI, a GPT-powered assistant that generates post copy, repurposes existing content, and suggests hashtags based on the brand's voice and past performance. Social listening covers brand mentions, sentiment analysis, and trend detection, powered by Talkwalker integration since Hootsuite acquired that technology. Analytics and reporting includes custom dashboards, competitor benchmarking, ROI tracking, and automated reporting. Engagement provides a unified inbox for all comments, messages, and mentions across platforms. Employee advocacy (the Amplify tool) makes it easy for employees to share approved content. Social commerce covers product tagging, shoppable posts, and social storefront management.

Hootsuite's AI features are the broadest in the category as of 2026: OwlyWriter AI for content generation, AI-powered best-time-to-post, automated sentiment analysis, content performance predictions, and smart inbox prioritizing. The platform integrates with over 200 apps including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Canva, and Adobe Creative Cloud. Pricing starts at $99/month for Professional (1 user, 10 accounts) and scales to Enterprise with custom pricing. Hootsuite competes with Sprout Social (deeper customer care features, higher starting price), Buffer (simpler, cheaper), and Sprinklr (full CX platform, enterprise-only). It fits mid-to-large marketing teams that need broad functionality (scheduling, listening, advocacy, commerce) from a single platform and are willing to pay more for that breadth compared to specialized tools.
