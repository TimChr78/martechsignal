Customer.io

Data-driven messaging platform for automated email, push, SMS, and in-app messages

Email Marketing · From $100/mo

Overview

Customer.io is a behavior-driven messaging platform that lets SaaS and digital product teams send targeted emails, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS based on how users actually interact with their product. Founded in 2012 and based in Portland, Oregon, it competes with Braze, Iterable, and OneSignal by focusing on event-triggered automation rather than list-based blasts. Teams pipe user events into Customer.io via a REST API or Segment integration, then build visual workflows that react in real time: a trial user who hasn't logged in for 3 days gets a re-engagement email, a power user who hits a milestone gets a congratulatory push. The platform recently rebranded its Campaign feature to Automations, reflecting the shift from scheduled sends to always-on behavioral triggers. Its Liquid-based templating supports dynamic content and personalization, and the segmentation engine allows complex filters based on user properties and event history. Pricing starts around $100/month for the Basic plan with email support; the Premium plan adds SMS, push, and in-app messaging at higher tiers. Customer.io's sweet spot is SaaS companies that have engineering resources to instrument events and want marketing workflows that respond to product usage, not just email clicks. The trade-off is that it assumes you have a data pipeline — it's not a turnkey email tool for non-technical teams who want to upload a CSV and start sending.
