MarketMuse

AI-powered content strategy and optimization platform for SEO content teams

SEO & Search · From $99/mo

Overview

MarketMuse is an AI content strategy and optimization platform that approaches SEO from the top down: instead of optimizing individual articles one at a time, it analyzes your entire site's content inventory, identifies topic gaps and opportunities, and scores your content against competitors across your full range of topics. Founded in 2015 and based in Montreal, MarketMuse competes with Clearscope and Surfer on content optimization but differentiates on its content planning layer above the article-level editor. Its core features include topic modeling that maps out the full semantic structure of a subject, content scoring that measures how comprehensively a page covers its topic, and competitive content audits that compare your site's coverage against competitors across hundreds of topics simultaneously. It also generates content briefs and initial drafts using AI, with the briefs incorporating not just keyword data but the full topical structure MarketMuse models. Pricing starts at $149/month for the Standard plan with basic topic research and content scoring; the Team plan at $399/month adds competitive audits, content inventory analysis, and collaboration features; the Premium plan at $999/month includes API access and dedicated support. MarketMuse's highest value is for established sites with large content libraries that need systematic gap analysis and content refresh prioritization. For teams producing fewer than 10 articles per month, the price premium over Frase or Surfer is hard to justify unless content strategy is a core differentiator.
