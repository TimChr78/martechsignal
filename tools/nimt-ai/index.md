Nimt.ai is an AI search tool from Sweden that combines tracking with an agent that does the fixing work. Tracking covers eight models (ChatGPT, ChatGPT Search, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude) and updates daily. The dashboard reports AI visibility, share of voice, ranking, sentiment, and an AI brand strength score. Citation tracking shows which pages and domains AI cites, with domain comparisons and query fan-out analysis for the sub-queries behind each answer. Competitor sets, projects, languages, and team members are unlimited on every plan, and coverage spans 230 countries.

The differentiator is the AI Search Agent. You delegate jobs to it in the web app or in Slack (Teams support is listed as coming soon), and it writes content, does on-page fixes and schema markup, runs off-page work such as PR and outreach, and takes on recurring jobs it performs without being asked. Changes wait for your approval before anything is published. A remote MCP server at api.nimt.ai/mcp exposes the same workspace to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, n8n, and other MCP clients, so agents can query your visibility data directly. The product also connects to 3,000+ tools including HubSpot, WordPress, Google Search Console, and Google Analytics.

Pricing is credit-based. New accounts get EUR 40 in free credits (a card is required), then the Flex plan costs EUR 79 per month for 10,000 credits and up to 72 tracked prompts per day. Everything the agent does spends credits, tracking steadily and content, audits, and outreach more heavily. Unused credits roll over and stay valid for two months. Enterprise is custom volume pricing on annual contracts with unlimited credits. Nimt was founded in Sweden by three co-founders from the agency and SaaS world.
