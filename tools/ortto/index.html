Ortto

AI-powered customer data and marketing automation platform with unified analytics

Marketing Automation · From $199/mo

Overview

Ortto is a marketing automation and customer data platform that evolved from Autopilot, the popular visual journey builder. The rebrand reflects a broader product vision: Ortto combines journey-based marketing automation with a built-in CDP and real-time analytics on a single platform, eliminating the need to stitch together separate tools for data, messaging, and measurement. The visual journey builder remains the centerpiece. Marketers drag and drop triggers, actions, and wait steps onto a canvas to create email, SMS, and push notification workflows. What changed from the Autopilot days is the data layer underneath. Ortto ingests customer data from websites, apps, CRMs, and third-party tools via API and JavaScript snippet, unifies it into single customer profiles, and feeds those profiles into the journey builder and the analytics dashboards. This means journeys fire based on actual behavior (product usage, page visits, purchase history) rather than just email clicks and form fills. AI features include predictive lead scoring, send-time optimization, and content suggestions. Pricing starts around $99/month for up to 2,500 contacts with basic features; the Business plan at $199/month adds the full CDP, multi-channel journeys, and advanced analytics. Ortto competes with ActiveCampaign and HubSpot in the SMB-to-mid-market space, differentiating on data unification and analytics depth. The main limitation is channel breadth: while Braze and Salesforce support 8+ messaging channels, Ortto focuses on email, SMS, and push, with in-app messaging as a recent addition.
