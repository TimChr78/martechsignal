Writer

Enterprise AI writing platform with brand governance, fact-checking, and agents

AI Content & Copywriting · From $18/mo

Overview

Writer is an enterprise AI platform built for organizations that treat brand consistency and regulatory compliance as hard requirements for AI adoption. Unlike tools that wrap third-party LLMs, Writer built its own Palmyra family of language models trained specifically on business and marketing language, with brand voice codification and content governance as core features. AI Studio, its application builder, lets teams create custom AI applications for specific marketing, sales, support, and operations workflows without writing code. The platform's defining feature is its style guide enforcement: companies upload their brand guidelines, terminology databases, and compliance rules, and the AI enforces these rules in every piece of generated content. Writer serves Fortune 500 companies including Intuit, L'Oreal, and Deloitte, and positions itself as the secure, governed alternative to ChatGPT Enterprise for companies that cannot risk AI generating off-brand or non-compliant content. Integrations include Chrome, Google Docs, Microsoft Word, Figma, and content management systems. Pricing is enterprise-only with custom quotes based on users and use cases. Writer competes with Jasper (marketing workflow focus) and Copy.ai (GTM workflow focus) but differentiates on its proprietary LLMs, governance depth, and suitability for heavily regulated industries like financial services, healthcare, and insurance, where the cost of an off-brand AI output is measured in regulatory fines rather than lost click-throughs.
