ProspectOS Review

Open-source lead prospecting CRM with Google Maps and Instagram scraping

CRM · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

ProspectOS finds small local businesses with no website or a bad one, then runs the outreach play for you. It scrapes Google Maps and Instagram for prospects, uses AI to draft the approach strategy and the first message, generates a PDF diagnostic you can send, and tracks every lead in a visual CRM through to close. Built on Flask and React with 230 passing tests, it is aimed at agencies and freelancers selling web services to local businesses. Version 2.0, actively developed.
