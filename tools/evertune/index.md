Evertune is a marketing platform for brand discovery in AI search. It was founded in 2024 by early team members of The Trade Desk and is headquartered in New York City. The product runs in four steps. Explore uses a Prompt Agent to build your tracking strategy from EverPanel, the company's consumer panel of 150 million real user prompts. Measure samples each prompt up to 100 times per model and benchmarks your brand against every competitor in the category across 11 AI models. Act turns visibility gaps into work. An Insights and Action Agent prioritizes the moves, Content Studio writes AI-optimized articles, a site audit checks AI crawler access, and Partner Connect places your brand on third-party sources that AI models already cite. Advertise covers the paid side. The Ads Agent buys ChatGPT ads in the conversations where you are missing and retargets users after they leave the AI chat. Measurement mixes two data sources: direct API calls that show how base models perceive your brand before any live search, and consumer app data that shows what live search returns. The gap between them reveals how far your SEO actually moves the answers.

Pricing has two tiers. Pro costs $800 per month and includes 100,000 prompts tracked across 11 AI models, 25 AI-optimized articles per month, three onboarding sessions, affiliate advertising partnerships, and AI retargeting. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds a dedicated AI Success Manager, customized onboarding, unlimited content generation, AI website optimization, AI bot analytics for your domain, and SSO. Both tiers are sold through a demo. In September 2026 Forrester named Evertune a Notable Vendor in its Q3 2026 answer engine optimization technologies report.
