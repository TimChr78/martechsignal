Overview

Evertune is a marketing platform for brand discovery in AI search. It was founded in 2024 by early team members of The Trade Desk and is headquartered in New York City. The product runs in four steps. Explore uses a Prompt Agent to build your tracking strategy from EverPanel, the company's consumer panel of 150 million real user prompts. Measure samples each prompt up to 100 times per model and benchmarks your brand against every competitor in the category across 11 AI models. Act turns visibility gaps into work. An Insights and Action Agent prioritizes the moves, Content Studio writes AI-optimized articles, a site audit checks AI crawler access, and Partner Connect places your brand on third-party sources that AI models already cite. Advertise covers the paid side. The Ads Agent buys ChatGPT ads in the conversations where you are missing and retargets users after they leave the AI chat. Measurement mixes two data sources: direct API calls that show how base models perceive your brand before any live search, and consumer app data that shows what live search returns. The gap between them reveals how far your SEO actually moves the answers. Pricing has two tiers. Pro costs $800 per month and includes 100,000 prompts tracked across 11 AI models, 25 AI-optimized articles per month, three onboarding sessions, affiliate advertising partnerships, and AI retargeting. Enterprise pricing is custom and adds a dedicated AI Success Manager, customized onboarding, unlimited content generation, AI website optimization, AI bot analytics for your domain, and SSO. Both tiers are sold through a demo. In September 2026 Forrester named Evertune a Notable Vendor in its Q3 2026 answer engine optimization technologies report.

Review notes

Assessed from Evertune's site, pricing page, company overview, and help center in September 2026.

Getting started is a sales process, not a signup. Pro includes three dedicated onboarding sessions, and Enterprise replaces those with customized onboarding plus a dedicated AI Success Manager. After that the work follows the site's four steps. The Prompt Agent proposes the prompts to track, drawing on topics from EverPanel, so the prompt set comes from observed consumer behavior instead of a brainstormed keyword list. You pick tracking frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly) and measurement runs each prompt up to 100 times per model. The dashboard reports a visibility score per model with competitor benchmarks and movement over time, along with sentiment and word association, source and citation analysis, and bot analysis of who crawls your domain.

The Act step is where the subscription turns into output. Pro caps you at 25 AI-optimized articles per month; Enterprise is unlimited. Content Studio writes them, the site audit flags blocked AI crawlers, and Partner Connect brokers placements on sources the models already cite. The Advertise step is the unusual part of this category. The Ads Agent sets up ChatGPT campaigns targeting conversations where your visibility is low, then retargets people after they exit the chat. The pricing page folds affiliate advertising partnerships and AI retargeting into Pro, but it does not say how media spend is billed on top of the subscription, so ask in the demo.

Two things separate this from single-sample trackers. Sampling 100 times per prompt gives scores with error bars rather than one lucky answer. The split between base model knowledge and live search responses shows whether your content is actually reaching the models. For teams that want GEO measurement tied to content and paid activation from one contract, that combination is rare at $800 per month.

Verdict

Evertune is one of the few GEO platforms that goes from measurement to media: it samples prompts across 11 AI models, writes content for the gaps, and buys ChatGPT ads where you are still missing. The Pro plan at $800 per month folds 25 AI-optimized articles into the subscription, so activation is not a separate purchase. Judge it as a GEO plus advertising platform, not a plain visibility dashboard.
