Smartly.io

AI-powered creative automation and media buying platform for enterprise advertisers

Advertising & Paid Media · Enterprise

Overview

Smartly.io is an enterprise AI advertising platform that combines creative automation, media buying optimization, and cross-platform campaign management in a single platform. Unlike tools that only generate ad creatives (AdCreative.ai, Pencil) or only automate media buying (Albert AI, Birch), Smartly.io covers the full advertising lifecycle: creative production, dynamic optimization, media buying, and analytics, all within one platform managed by AI that orchestrates ads across Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Google Ads, and programmatic display. The platform's capabilities span three pillars. Creative Automation runs Dynamic Creative Optimization at scale: Smartly's DCO engine combines product feeds, brand templates, and audience data to generate thousands of personalized ad variations, automatically testing and optimizing creative elements per audience segment. Media and Campaign Management uses autonomous AI to handle bid optimization, budget allocation, audience targeting, and cross-channel pacing, shifting spend to the highest-performing channels and pausing underperformers in real time. Intelligence and Analytics covers cross-channel attribution, creative performance analytics down to the individual element level, automated reporting, and predictive budget recommendations. A key differentiator is Smartly's Predictive Budget Allocation (PBA), where the AI forecasts channel-level performance and recommends (or automatically executes) budget shifts to maximize ROAS across the full media mix. Smartly.io is firmly enterprise-tier. The platform targets advertisers spending $500K+/month on paid media, with platform fees typically 3-5% of managed ad spend. It serves global brands including Uber, eBay, Under Armour, and L'Oreal, and has strategic partnerships with Meta, TikTok, and Google as a certified partner. It competes with Albert AI (autonomous optimization, less creative production), Pencil (creative governance, less media buying), and Marin Software (enterprise paid search, less creative and social). Smartly.io suits large-scale enterprise advertisers and agencies that want to consolidate creative production and media buying into one AI-managed platform, reducing the tool stack from 4-5 separate platforms (creative tool, DCO tool, bid management, analytics, reporting) to one. Its main limitation is the price: at enterprise spend levels, the platform fee is a meaningful line item, and smaller advertisers will find tools like AdCreative.ai plus Birch more cost-effective for similar, if less integrated, results.
