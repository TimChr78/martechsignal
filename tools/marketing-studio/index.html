Marketing Studio Review

Agent-driven marketing studio for Claude Code: launch assets from one command

Agent Skills · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Marketing Studio turns Claude Code into a launch-asset engine. Type /marketing in your product repo and the agent onboards your brand, captures your app with Playwright, and renders a complete asset suite: a Blender logo reveal, product demo videos with camera moves and cursor tracking, a 30-90 second launch video, social clips, and animated OG images. Everything renders from brand tokens alone, and the finished files copy back to your repo. It exists because launch assets are the last manual step of shipping, and one command beats a week of Figma and screen recording.

AI Capabilities

  • Social clip creation
  • OG asset generation
