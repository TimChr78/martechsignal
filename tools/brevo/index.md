## Overview

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is a Paris-based multichannel marketing platform whose core billing trick is pricing by monthly email volume rather than contact count, which keeps large contact databases cheap for teams that send infrequently. Email campaigns, SMS, WhatsApp, push, live chat, chatbot, and phone calls share one contact universe, and the built-in CRM adds pipelines, tasks, and a sales add-on seat package on top. The free plan covers 300 emails a day; paid plans raise volume and unlock automation workflows, A/B testing, landing pages, send-time optimization, and web event tracking. A 2026 addition aimed at the agentic market: an MCP server lets teams manage campaigns and contacts directly from ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI tools, and Brevo itself ships a Brevo Data Platform that unifies customer data across channels. Integrations cover Shopify, WordPress, Stripe, Zapier, Salesforce, WooCommerce, and 150+ more in the directory; transactional email rides the same platform through SMTP relay and API. The trade-offs match the model: the contact universe is generous but email credits are the meter, SMS costs credits on every plan, advanced analytics and heatmaps need Standard and up, and Editorial verdicts elsewhere note reporting is shallower than Klaviyo's revenue-data model. This assessment is based on the vendor's documented architecture and public materials.

## Pricing

Brevo is freemium, with a free tier to start, paid plans start at $9/mo as of 2026-10.

Free plan (300 emails/day); Starter from $9/mo at 5,000 emails; Standard $18/mo at 5,000; Professional $499/mo; Enterprise custom; SMS credits sold separately (figures per 2026-09-29 third-party receipt; vendor pricing page renders price numbers only via JavaScript)
