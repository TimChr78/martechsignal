Krayin CRM

Free open-source Laravel CRM for SMEs and enterprises with full customer lifecycle management

CRM · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Krayin CRM is an open-source Laravel CRM that covers the fundamentals and stops there. Built on Laravel (the PHP framework powering a significant chunk of the web), Krayin handles leads, contacts, organizations, pipeline management, activities, and email integration. What Krayin gets right is the Laravel-native approach. If your team already runs a Laravel application, Krayin slots into your stack without introducing a new language, database, or deployment model. The codebase is MIT-licensed and hosted on GitHub, so you can fork it, brand it, and extend it without licensing friction. A multi-tenant SaaS edition exists for teams running multiple business units from a single deployment, a feature most open-source CRMs skip. The limitations are straightforward: Krayin is a traditional CRM, not an AI-powered one. No built-in lead scoring, no predictive analytics, no workflow automation engine. Email integration is basic IMAP/SMTP, not the deep Gmail/Outlook sync of HubSpot or Attio. The UI is functional Laravel admin-panel style, clean but not modern by 2026 standards. Third-party integrations are sparse compared to commercial CRMs; you'll be building most connections yourself. Krayin is best for Laravel shops that want a self-hosted CRM they can extend in PHP, teams with strong privacy requirements where data must never leave their servers, and organizations that need a basic pipeline manager without the SaaS tax. It is not for teams expecting AI features, plug-and-play integrations, or a polished user experience out of the box. Think of it as a CRM starter kit rather than a finished product.
