Matomo

Open-source web analytics platform with full data ownership and AI-powered insights

Open-Source Tools · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Wellington, New Zealand, Matomo is an open-source web analytics platform designed to give organizations full ownership of their visitor data. It tracks websites, e-commerce stores, and apps while providing reports on traffic sources, user behavior, conversions, and campaign performance. Because the core product is available under the GPL, teams can self-host it for free, use Matomo Cloud from €19 per month, or add premium plugins for extended capabilities. The project has 21,717 GitHub stars and includes an API, with integrations for WordPress, Shopify, Magento, Zapier, Google Analytics import, Slack, and tag managers. Matomo is aimed at marketing, analytics, product, and privacy teams that want more control than typical hosted analytics services provide. Its main differentiator is the combination of open-source transparency and a broad analytics suite, including AI-powered insights, AI anomaly detection, AI form analytics, AI heatmaps, and AI session replay. These features help users surface unusual trends, understand where visitors hesitate, and identify friction in forms or journeys without relying solely on manual report review. Compared with commercial alternatives such as Google Analytics or Adobe Analytics, Matomo places greater emphasis on data ownership, self-hosting flexibility, and customizable reporting, though organizations may need more technical resources to manage infrastructure and plugins. It is best for privacy-conscious businesses, agencies, e-commerce teams, and enterprises that want an open-source analytics platform with AI-assisted insights and the option to move from self-hosted to cloud-based deployment.
