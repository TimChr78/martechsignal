Workato

Enterprise integration and automation platform with AI-powered workflow orchestration

Workflow Automation · Enterprise

Overview

Workato positions itself as "The Control and Execution Platform for Enterprise AI," and it actually separates those two concerns. The Control Plane governs every agent action (RBAC, audit logs, data masking, human approval gates). The Execution Plane does the work (14,000+ app connectors, event triggers, composable MCP servers, orchestration engine). This dual-plane architecture solves a real problem: most platforms either govern well but can't execute, or execute well but have no governance. Workato argues you need both on one platform, or you get two systems with a gap in between. A 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader position backs up the enterprise credibility. The platform has evolved well beyond classic iPaaS. Agentic capabilities let AI agents trigger workflows directly, so they act on data rather than just reading it. Enterprise MCP servers make your internal APIs discoverable to agents. Low-code apps and workflow bots extend automation to business users without developer bottlenecks. Pricing starts at $833/month and scales to $15,000+/month for enterprise deployments. Like Tray, this is enterprise territory. Workato competes with Boomi, MuleSoft, and SAP Integration Suite, not Zapier or Make. For the 1,000+ developer enterprise where marketing ops needs governed, auditable AI automation that can scale across departments, Workato is the reference architecture rather than the budget option.
