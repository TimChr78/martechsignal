Persado

Motivation AI platform generating emotionally optimized marketing copy at scale

AI Content & Copywriting · Enterprise

Overview

Persado is an enterprise AI content optimization platform that takes a fundamentally different approach to AI-generated marketing copy: instead of using LLMs to produce creative text from a prompt, it uses a proprietary Motivation AI engine that understands which language elements drive emotional response and action in specific audience segments. Founded in 2012 and headquartered in New York, Persado targets regulated industries where marketing language carries compliance and reputational risk: financial services, retail banking, insurance, travel, and hospitality. Its platform spans four layers: content generation informed by a taxonomy of emotional language elements, performance prediction that scores content before deployment, optimization that tests variants, and delivery to email, web, mobile, and ad platforms. The system learns which words, phrases, emotions, and formatting patterns drive conversion for specific audiences and applies those patterns to future content. Compliance and brand safety are built in: all generated content must pass regulatory and brand checks before going live. Persado is enterprise-only with custom pricing; customers include JPMorgan Chase, Dell, and major airlines. It competes with Phrasee in the enterprise language optimization space but differentiates on its emotional intelligence taxonomy and depth of service layer for regulated marketing organizations.
