## Overview

Persado is an AI content platform for regulated marketing, and its 2026 site leads with new framing: the agentic creative agency for regulated brands, aimed squarely at financial services. The product surface is three named pillars, Create (generate compliant content at speed and scale), Optimize (score and improve every message before it ships), and Automate (personalize every send automatically via API), plus two 2025 launches: Persado Dynamic Email, which the company says cuts email operations time by up to 75%, and Persado Marketing Compliance AI, positioned as the first agentic AI platform built for financial services marketing and legal teams. Underneath sits a Performance Knowledge Base the company describes as 100,000+ message elements, a proprietary taxonomy categorized by emotional drivers, CTA types, and performance attributes, drawing on what the site claims is 1M+ A/B tests, 120K+ campaigns, and scoring against 150B+ customer interactions. Compliance is the sales argument: 20+ regulatory frameworks built into generation, including UDAAP, TILA, Reg Z, ECOA, and TCPA, brand voice guardrails, audit trails, and a stated zero post-deployment compliance incidents across 10+ years and 8 of the 10 largest U.S. banks. Channels are email (full message: subject, preheader, body, CTA), direct mail, web banners, social ads, SMS, and push through partner platforms, delivered via 121 documented integrations, 27 of them email and messaging platforms: Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Campaign Classic and Journey Optimizer, Braze, Oracle Eloqua and Responsys, and SAP Emarsys among them. Publicly named customers are banks: Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Ally, NatWest, Discover, TD Bank, LendingClub, and M&amp;T Bank. Founded in 2012, headquartered in New York, and independent as Persado Inc. under co-founders Alex Vratskides and Assaf Baciu, it sells through demo requests only, with onboarding quoted at roughly four weeks and no published pricing.

## How to install

- No trial and no published pricing: the documented entry path is the demo form at persado.com/contact, which offers Sales, Press or Media, Job, Agency/Partner, and Other inquiry types.
- Onboarding is quoted at approximately four weeks. For the enterprise managed service a dedicated Persado team handles setup, integration, and initial campaign delivery; a self-service Studio tier exists for Optimize only and is described as faster to stand up.
- The Salesforce Marketing Cloud integration documents four connection models with different lead times: Native (2-3 weeks including QA), Data Integration Pipeline (4-6 weeks), Service-Delivered (live on the first campaign cycle), and a Chrome extension that optimizes copy in any web UI with no IT involvement.
- Customers sign in at portal.persado.com; there is no public sandbox or evaluation environment.
- The site also documents an MCP surface at persado.com/mcp with its own privacy policy, for connecting the platform to AI assistants.
## Requirements

Enterprise message volume worth optimizing, an existing ESP or CDP to deliver through (the integration catalog lists 121), and a procurement cycle: pricing is quote-based, the only self-serve option is Studio for Optimize, and onboarding is measured in weeks rather than hours.

## Best for

High-volume financial services marketers, card issuers, banks, credit unions, and lenders that need every message pre-scored against regulatory frameworks before it ships, and that send enough for percentage lift to be worth enterprise money.

## Not for

Small teams and non-regulated verticals: there is no trial, no published price, the documented verticals are all financial services, and the Chrome extension is the only low-commitment way to see the scoring. Buyers wanting a self-serve copywriting tool should look elsewhere.

## Review notes

Researched from public documentation, the source repository, and vendor materials. Not a hands-on test.

Researched from persado.com, its press releases, and its integration pages (September 2026). Not a hands-on review. The biggest change is positioning: the site now leads with the agentic creative agency for regulated brands rather than the Motivation AI Platform language our record used, though Motivation AI still appears in press releases and case studies.

Three claims needed correcting. Our vertical list included travel and hospitality, but the current site sells financial services verticals only (card acquisition, mortgage and lending, consumer banking, co-brand, credit unions, insurance and wealth, fintech), with travel mentioned only as a past customer category. Our customer list named JPMorgan Chase, Dell, and major airlines: Chase is documented in a 2019 five-year deal announcement that also cites a 450% ad click-through lift in its pilot, Dell appears only in 2021-and-earlier boilerplate, and no airline is named anywhere on the live site. Our integration list also needed precision, since generic entries like Salesforce, Adobe, Oracle, and SAP map to specific documented products.

The performance numbers are the vendor&#x27;s own and are large: 1M+ A/B tests, 120K+ campaigns, 150B+ customer interactions scored, a claimed 96% correlation between predicted and actual campaign performance, and $2.5B in attributed revenue for financial services brands in a September 2024 press release. One attestation could not be verified: the navigation advertises SOC 2 Type II while the platform security page claims ISO 27001 alignment and SOC 1 coverage through its AWS hosting, and we found no page stating Persado itself holds a SOC 2 Type II report.

Ownership is a recurring confusion worth settling: Persado is independent. It operates as Persado Inc. with co-founders Alex Vratskides (CEO) and Assaf Baciu (President) still leading it, and no documented acquisition or Accenture relationship, with the most recent independent press release dated October 8, 2025.
