Budibase Review

Open-source operations platform for building AI agents, apps and automations on your own data

Workflow Automation · Free tier · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Budibase is an open-source operations platform for building internal apps, automations and, since its 2.x releases, AI agents that act on operational data. You connect databases and APIs, design interfaces in a visual builder, and wire up multi-step automations; self-hosting is free under GPLv3, with paid cloud and business tiers for hosting, SSO and support. For marketing operations the practical builds are the internal systems generic SaaS covers badly: lead-routing consoles, campaign budget trackers, content approval queues and dashboards that join ad platform data with CRM records. Because it is self-hosted, lead data and consent records stay inside your own infrastructure, which matters for EU teams with GDPR obligations. The 2.x direction adds agents that work over those same data connections, positioned as model-agnostic. The honest trade-offs: data modeling is part of the job, the AI features are young, and marketing teams without any technical help will feel the friction that engineering teams don't. Against NocoBase, its closest peer in this directory, Budibase is faster to a working CRUD app but less deep on data-model-driven structure. At 28,000-plus GitHub stars with daily development it is one of the most active projects in this class. We have not run Budibase in production; this assessment is based on the documented architecture and public materials.
