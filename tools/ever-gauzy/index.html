Ever Gauzy Review

Ever® Gauzy™ - Open Business Management Platform (ERP/CRM/HRM/ATS/PM) - https://gauzy.co

CRM · Open Source · OPEN SOURCE

Overview

Ever Gauzy is an open business management platform covering ERP, CRM, HRM, ATS for recruiting, project management, and employee time-tracking under one AGPLv3 codebase. The scope is unusual for open source: most competitors pick one lane, Gauzy bundles HR-adjacent operations with client management, aimed at collaborative, on-demand, and sharing-economy businesses. The stack is Node.js and NestJS with React (Next.js) frontends, an exposed public API at api.gauzy.co, and a companion Ever Teams product for work management that connects to the same platform APIs. Docker deployment is documented, and the company behind it (Ever Co) sells enterprise support around the open core. For a marketing team the realistic entry point is the CRM plus time-and-activity tracking combination, since the ERP depth targets finance workflows few marketing stacks need. At 4,300-plus stars it is one of the larger projects in this directory, and the feature breadth is real but shallow in places: treat it as a broad platform where you adopt two or three modules, not a suite where you adopt everything.

Hands-on notes

Ever Gauzy bundles ERP, CRM, HRM with time tracking, and ATS recruiting in one NestJS-based platform - a scope no other open-source project matches. The time-tracking module is the most polished surface, with desktop timers and activity monitoring aimed at distributed teams.

The employee-monitoring heritage is visible and worth explicit team conversations before rollout. Being a monorepo of many modules, self-hosting demands more operational commitment than single-purpose tools. Organizations wanting one system across operations and HR, willing to run it, get unusual value.

Verdict

Unusually broad open-source business platform; best value where time tracking anchors the rollout.
