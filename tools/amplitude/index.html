Amplitude

AI-powered digital analytics platform for product and marketing teams

Analytics & Attribution · Freemium

Overview

Amplitude is the leading product analytics platform, ranked #1 in Product Analytics on G2 for 23 consecutive quarters (as of Spring 2026). It provides a unified digital analytics suite covering **product analytics** (event tracking, cohort analysis, funnel reports, user segmentation), **experimentation** (A/B testing with statistical rigor), **session replay** (visual user journey reconstruction), and **customer data activation** (audience syncing to marketing and engagement tools). Amplitude's core strength is its event-based data model that tracks every user action (clicks, views, transactions) as granular events with properties, letting teams answer questions like "which feature drives retention?" and "where do power users come from?" without writing SQL. The platform's AI capabilities include **Amplitude AI** for automated insight discovery, anomaly detection, and predictive analytics that surface "unknown unknowns" in user behavior. Amplitude's **Warehouse Native** architecture allows querying data directly in Snowflake or Databricks without data duplication, which matters for enterprises with existing data warehouse investments. The platform serves over 2,500 customers including Atlassian, NBCUniversal, PayPal, and Ford. Pricing is event-volume based with a generous free tier (up to 50K MTUs) and paid plans starting around $50/month for Starter. Amplitude competes primarily with Mixpanel (event analytics, smaller-scale focus), Heap (auto-capture analytics, owned by Contentsquare), and PostHog (open-source alternative). It is best suited for product-led growth companies and enterprises that need a dedicated product analytics platform with experimentation built in.

Product-led growth teams that need event analytics, funnels, and experimentation in one place, and enterprises that want warehouse-native querying against Snowflake or Databricks.

Not for

Teams that mainly need channel-level marketing reporting; GA4 covers acquisitions and campaigns at zero cost. Amplitude earns its price when product behavior questions drive decisions.

Hands-on notes

Amplitude is product analytics: events, funnels, retention, and now AI-generated insights. The strength is the event model, which lets you ask product questions without SQL. Funnel analysis and behavioral cohorts are fast, and the dashboards are the standard in the product world. The learning curve is real but shallow enough for a data-literate marketer.

Pricing has a generous free tier and scales steeply past it. The AI layer summarizes anomalies and flags drops, which saves the 'why did conversions fall' hunt.

Verdict

Standard choice for product-led companies. For pure marketing analytics, Google Analytics 4 covers more channels with less cost.

Does Amplitude have heatmaps?

Not as classic click overlays. Amplitude's closest equivalents are session replay and journey analysis, which reconstruct where users struggle after the fact. Dedicated scroll-and-click heatmap overlays are not core; tools like Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity fill that gap.

What is Amplitude used for?

Product analytics. It tracks every user action as an event so teams can answer which features drive retention, where users abandon funnels, and which cohorts convert. The platform also bundles A/B testing and behavioral recommendations.

Is Amplitude free?

There's a free plan covering up to 2M events per month with core analytics. Paid plans start at $49/month (Plus), with Growth and Enterprise tiers priced by event volume and features like session replay volume.
