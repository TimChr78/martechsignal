Amplitude

AI-powered digital analytics platform for product and marketing teams

Analytics & Attribution · Freemium

Overview

Amplitude is the leading product analytics platform, ranked #1 in Product Analytics on G2 for 23 consecutive quarters (as of Spring 2026). It provides a unified digital analytics suite covering **product analytics** (event tracking, cohort analysis, funnel reports, user segmentation), **experimentation** (A/B testing with statistical rigor), **session replay** (visual user journey reconstruction), and **customer data activation** (audience syncing to marketing and engagement tools). Amplitude's core strength is its event-based data model that tracks every user action (clicks, views, transactions) as granular events with properties, letting teams answer questions like "which feature drives retention?" and "where do power users come from?" without writing SQL. The platform's AI capabilities include **Amplitude AI** for automated insight discovery, anomaly detection, and predictive analytics that surface "unknown unknowns" in user behavior. Amplitude's **Warehouse Native** architecture allows querying data directly in Snowflake or Databricks without data duplication, which matters for enterprises with existing data warehouse investments. The platform serves over 2,500 customers including Atlassian, NBCUniversal, PayPal, and Ford. Pricing is event-volume based with a generous free tier (up to 50K MTUs) and paid plans starting around $50/month for Starter. Amplitude competes primarily with Mixpanel (event analytics, smaller-scale focus), Heap (auto-capture analytics, owned by Contentsquare), and PostHog (open-source alternative). It is best suited for product-led growth companies and enterprises that need a dedicated product analytics platform with experimentation built in.
