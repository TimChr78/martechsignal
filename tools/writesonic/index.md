The AI Search Growth Engine: GEO tracking, AI articles, and site audits in one platform

Writesonic positions itself as The AI Search Growth Engine, and its GEO product for brands is what earns the name. The platform tracks how AI answers mention your brand, then produces the articles and site audits meant to fix the gaps. Founded in 2021 by Samanyou Garg and part of Y Combinator's S21 batch, the company started as an AI writing tool and reoriented around AI search; the pricing page now leads with prompt and answer tracking rather than copy generation.

Plans run in four tiers, priced per month and billed annually. Starter at $79 tracks 50 prompts and 50 answers daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, and adds 15 AI articles, 10 site audits of 100 pages each, and ads monitoring. Basic at $199 raises tracking to 100 prompts and 300 answers and includes 25 articles. Growth at $399 covers 200 prompts and 600 answers, adds sentiment analysis and a trial of the Action Center, and ships 50 articles. Enterprise is custom and opens the full surface: all 10 AI platforms (adding Perplexity, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI, and Google AI Mode), the full Action Center, AI search volume and trend insights, shopping and agentic commerce, SSO/SAML, and SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance. Agentic workflows run on a trial basis on the lower tiers.

The integration story is wider than most GEO tools: 20+ native connections across CMS, CDN, and analytics, including WordPress, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, and Looker Studio, plus a public API and an MCP server. A free trial needs no credit card. Writesonic, Inc. runs as a remote team with a San Francisco address.
