Writesonic Review

The AI Search Growth Engine: GEO tracking, AI articles, and site audits in one platform

GEO & LLM Optimization · From $79/mo

MartechSignal editorial review by Tim Christensen · updated 2026-09-25

Overview

Writesonic positions itself as The AI Search Growth Engine, and its GEO product for brands is what earns the name. The platform tracks how AI answers mention your brand, then produces the articles and site audits meant to fix the gaps. Founded in 2021 by Samanyou Garg and part of Y Combinator's S21 batch, the company started as an AI writing tool and reoriented around AI search; the pricing page now leads with prompt and answer tracking rather than copy generation. Plans run in four tiers, priced per month and billed annually. Starter at $79 tracks 50 prompts and 50 answers daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, and adds 15 AI articles, 10 site audits of 100 pages each, and ads monitoring. Basic at $199 raises tracking to 100 prompts and 300 answers and includes 25 articles. Growth at $399 covers 200 prompts and 600 answers, adds sentiment analysis and a trial of the Action Center, and ships 50 articles. Enterprise is custom and opens the full surface: all 10 AI platforms (adding Perplexity, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI, and Google AI Mode), the full Action Center, AI search volume and trend insights, shopping and agentic commerce, SSO/SAML, and SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance. Agentic workflows run on a trial basis on the lower tiers. The integration story is wider than most GEO tools: 20+ native connections across CMS, CDN, and analytics, including WordPress, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, and Looker Studio, plus a public API and an MCP server. A free trial needs no credit card. Writesonic, Inc. runs as a remote team with a San Francisco address.

Review notes

Assessed from Writesonic's pricing, integrations, and about pages in September 2026. We have not run a campaign through the platform.

The trial starts without a card, and the first task is prompt setup. Starter tracks 50 prompts and 50 answers per day across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. From there the loop is diagnose, produce, deploy. Site audits run in batches sized by tier (100 pages per audit on Starter, 1,200 on Basic, 2,500 on Growth), AI articles arrive in monthly quotas of 15, 25, or 50, and ads monitoring watches sponsored placements in AI answers.

The tier design shapes the experience more than the feature list does. Lower plans track three engines; all 10 are an Enterprise feature. Sentiment analysis appears at Growth, and the Action Center, the part that turns findings into on-page and off-page actions, is gated at Enterprise, with a trial allocation of five off-page and five on-page actions per month on Growth. Agentic workflow runs are metered too: 10 on Starter, 50 on Basic, 100 on Growth. So on the entry plan you get measurement plus content production, and the execution layer waits for a bigger contract.

Deployment is where Writesonic parts company with dashboard-only trackers. Integrations push fixes into CMSs such as WordPress, Sanity, and Contentful, pull data from Search Console and Ahrefs, and ship through CDN edges such as Vercel, Fastly, Cloudflare, and Akamai. The public API and MCP server open the same data to agents, and Enterprise plans add SSO/SAML alongside SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR coverage. For a brand team that wants visibility data, articles, and audits in one subscription, the Starter plan is the shortest path in this batch.

Verdict

Writesonic is the entry-level option in this batch that still bundles measurement, content, and audits: $79 per month billed annually buys daily tracking and 15 AI articles alongside the visibility dashboard. The limits are tier gates rather than missing features, since full engine coverage, sentiment, and the Action Center sit at Growth and Enterprise. It fits brands that want one subscription for GEO diagnosis and production and can live with three engines to start.
