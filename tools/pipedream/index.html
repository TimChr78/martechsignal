Pipedream

Workflow Automation · Paid

Overview

Pipedream is a developer-first integration and automation platform that connects over 3,000 APIs, AI models, databases, and services through code-level workflows. Founded in 2019 and acquired by Workday in 2026, Pipedream serves over one million developers from startups to Fortune 500 companies including LinkedIn, Scale AI, and Warner Bros. Discovery. Its platform spans four product surfaces: a visual Workflow Builder where steps can be no-code integrations or arbitrary Node.js, Python, or Go code; Connect, an SDK that lets product teams embed Pipedream's 3,000+ integrations into their own applications or AI agents with managed OAuth; MCP servers that deploy integrations as tool endpoints consumable by any Model Context Protocol client including Claude, Cursor, and Copilot; and String, an AI agent builder where users describe what they want in natural language and Pipedream generates, deploys, and hosts the agent. The platform includes 10,000 pre-built triggers and actions, a built-in key-value data store, file storage, concurrency controls, and GitHub Sync for version-controlled workflow deployment across environments. Security certifications include SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA compliance, and GDPR. Pipedream uses a credit-based pricing model where you pay for compute time: the Free tier includes 100 credits per month with 1 million AI tokens, 3 active workflows, and 3 connected accounts. The Basic plan at $29/month includes 2,000 credits and 20 million AI tokens; the Advanced plan at $49/month adds unlimited workflows and accounts, control flow operators (branching, parallelism, switch), premium apps, and GitHub Sync; the Connect plan at $99/month is for teams embedding integrations in their own products with managed auth for up to 100 external users. Business plans are custom-quoted with volume pricing, dedicated Slack support, HIPAA workloads, and SLAs. Pipedream competes with Zapier, Make, n8n, Tray.io, and Workato in the automation space, but differentiates on its developer-first flexibility: unlike pure no-code tools, every step can run arbitrary code, and unlike pure infrastructure tools like AWS Lambda, it provides managed auth, a pre-built integration catalog, and an execution environment out of the box. Its MCP server deployment capability is unique in the category: marketers or developers can turn any Pipedream workflow into an MCP server endpoint that AI coding agents can call directly, making it a bridge between traditional iPaaS automation and agentic workflows. The Workday acquisition signals a move toward deeper enterprise integration, particularly around HR, finance, and planning systems where Workday is dominant. For marketing and revenue operations teams, Pipedream's strongest use cases are data pipelines between martech tools (routing leads from ad platforms to CRMs, enriching contact data across systems, syncing campaign metrics to dashboards), AI-powered content workflows (triggering content generation or analysis from webhook events), and building internal tools that combine multiple marketing APIs into single endpoints.
