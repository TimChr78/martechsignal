Email Marketing Tools

Email campaigns, sequences, and deliverability

Email marketing remains the channel with the highest measurable ROI and the lowest tolerance for error. This category splits into campaign senders, lifecycle automation engines, and deliverability specialists. The AI layer now drafts subject lines, predicts send times, and segments lists automatically. Deliverability is where tools truly differ. inbox placement depends on infrastructure choices most vendors do not advertise. We test sending mechanics, template flexibility, and how each platform handles list hygiene without you noticing.
