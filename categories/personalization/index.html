Personalization & CDP Tools

Customer data platforms and experience personalization

Website personalization tools change what each visitor sees based on who they are or what they just did. The pitch is simple: a returning customer should not see the same homepage as a first-time visitor. In practice most teams use these platforms for three jobs. swapping hero content by segment, adjusting product recommendations, and gating offers behind behavior. We test how well each tool handles identity resolution, how quickly segments update after an action, and whether the targeting rules survive contact with a real campaign calendar.
