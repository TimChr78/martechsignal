CRM Tools

Customer relationship management and sales pipelines

Systems of record for customer relationships and sales pipelines. We look at AI scoring, data hygiene, per-seat economics, and what self-hosting costs you in practice. This is the deepest category in the directory: nineteen systems, fourteen of them open source or freemium, with usable entry points from $14 per month down to free self-hosting. That abundance is the trap. Open-source CRMs differ less on features than on upgrade discipline, API completeness, and whether the community would survive if the founding company vanished. Our hands-on notes focus on migration paths and what breaks after year one.
