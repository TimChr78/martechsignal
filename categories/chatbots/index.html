Chatbots & Conversational AI Tools

AI chatbots, virtual assistants, and conversational marketing

Chatbot and conversational AI tools handle the front line of customer conversation: answering common questions, routing requests, and capturing leads when humans are offline. The category has split in two. Rule-based bots still run FAQ deflection reliably. LLM-based agents now draft their own answers, which raises the stakes on guardrails: a confident wrong answer costs more than no answer. Our reviews focus on escalation quality, handoff to humans, and what happens when the bot meets a question its training did not cover.
