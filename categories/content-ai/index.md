Content AI is where most teams meet generative AI first. Drafts are cheap and fast. The scarce parts are taste, fact checks, and a clear voice. This set covers 13 tools across that full path, from first draft to published page. Five are open source and self-hostable. The rest run paid, freemium, or enterprise plans, with entry points from free tiers and $9 per month up to quote-based enterprise deals.

Start with the job, not the model. If you need daily ads, blogs, and social posts, the copywriting workhorses fit. If legal or brand reviews every word, look at the governed enterprise group. If you publish on your own domain, Ghost and Strapi hold the stack. If you want helpers you run yourself, the self-hosted utilities cover grammar, research, and translation. Keep an approval step in place and check what the tool actually changed before it ships. Every entry below names its price model and license so the shortlist takes minutes.

IF You need daily marketing copy and want to start cheap

[Copy.ai](/tools/copy-ai/) [ContentBot](/tools/contentbot/) [Jasper](/tools/jasper/)

Copy.ai has a free plan at 2,000 words per month and Pro at $49 per month or $36 annual; ContentBot starts at $9 per month with a prepaid per-word option at $0.50 per 1K words; Jasper Creator starts at $39 annual or $49 monthly

IF You need scored or governed copy with brand control

[Anyword](/tools/anyword/) [Writer](/tools/writer/)

Anyword pairs copywriting with predictive performance scores from $39 per month annual; Writer is quote-based with Palmyra models, brand governance, and agents for teams that need control

IF You work in regulated or high-stakes messaging

[Persado](/tools/persado/) [Phrasee](/tools/phrasee/)

Persado is enterprise custom pricing focused on finserv, retail, and travel; Phrasee, now Jacquard, is enterprise quote-based with no trial, built for message testing at scale

IF You want to own the publishing stack

[Ghost](/tools/ghost/) [Strapi](/tools/strapi/)

Ghost is MIT self-hosted free with Cloud from $9 per month; Strapi is MIT self-hosted free with Cloud Developer free and paid tiers from $99 per month

IF You want free self-hosted writing utilities

[LanguageTool](/tools/languagetool/) [Khoj](/tools/khoj/) [LibreTranslate](/tools/libretranslate/)

LanguageTool is free self-hosted with Premium from SEK 49.96 per month; Khoj is free self-hosted for research and writing over your own docs; LibreTranslate is free self-hosted translation with hosted API billed per character
