Social Media Tools

Social scheduling, listening, and engagement

Social media tools span scheduling, listening, and analytics across the major networks. Publishing is a solved problem; the interesting differences are in listening depth and response workflows. The newer generation adds AI drafting and sentiment classification, which helps volume and raises questions about voice consistency. We look at network API coverage (several tools quietly lost access to key platforms), approval flows for teams, and whether reporting connects back to actual business outcomes.
