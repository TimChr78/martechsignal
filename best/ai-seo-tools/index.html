Best AI SEO tools (2026)

This list is for content and SEO teams buying AI tooling in 2026: people who need briefs drafted, pages scored, and a measurable read on whether ChatGPT or Google AI cites their brand. Every pick has documented AI features in the catalog, not an AI label on a marketing page.

The category splits in two along a line worth knowing before you shop. Content-optimization tools research, brief, draft, and score pages against what already ranks. Visibility tools measure what answer engines say about you. Several products now cover both halves, and the pricing shapes differ accordingly.

When buying, check three things. Which AI features are included versus metered, since drafts and AI steps are commonly capped or billed separately. Whether the tool researches topics or only optimizes against a keyword you chose. And what the visibility coverage lists, since tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews is not a given on entry plans.

Frase

Frase relaunched in 2026 as what its docs call an agentic SEO and GEO platform, and it is materially different from the brief-and-editor tool older reviews describe. The loop runs: connect a site, audit up to your plan's page allowance, then let Frase Agent research a topic, draft in your brand voice, and publish through WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, or Wix. Content scores three ways on a 0 to 100 scale (EEAT, GEO, SEO), Content Guard monitors published pages with confidence-tagged fixes, and AI Visibility tracks cited URLs and share of voice across ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter, adding Perplexity on Professional and Claude and Gemini on Scale. Starter costs 39 dollars monthly billed yearly (49 dollars monthly, 1 seat, 10 articles), Professional 103 dollars, Scale 239 dollars. Every plan includes API, MCP, and CLI allowances.

Verdict: Best for research, drafting, publishing, and AI visibility in one loop.

Skip it if you need deep backlink or keyword-database research.

Surfer SEO

Surfer SEO is the on-page optimization specialist: its Content Editor processes more than 500 on-page signals, analyzes the top 50 ranking pages for a keyword, and builds a content score from term frequency, heading structure, word count, and image count while you write, flagging missing and overused terms in real time. Surfer AI generates optimized drafts that score 60 to 70 or better on its own scale directly in the editor. Around that sit Keyword Research, SERP Analyzer, Content Planner, and Domain Planner modules, with Google Docs, WordPress, and Jasper integrations. Catalog pricing starts at 49 dollars monthly (Discovery), runs through Standard at 99 to 119 dollars and Pro at 182 to 219 dollars, topping out at Peace of Mind for 299 to 359 dollars. The documented criticism is focus: topic strategy still needs a research platform.

Verdict: Best for optimizing pages against live SERP patterns at scale.

Skip it if keyword discovery matters more than page optimization.

Semrush

Semrush is the all-in-one platform: keyword research, competitive analysis, rank tracking, site auditing, content optimization, link building, paid ad intelligence, and AI search visibility under one subscription. The catalog records a keyword database of 25 billion keywords across 140-plus countries and a backlink index processing trillions of URLs. Core surfaces include Domain Overview, the Keyword Magic Tool, daily Position Tracking, a Site Audit crawler with 140-plus checks, and a Content Marketing Toolkit with topic research and templates. Documented AI covers a writing assistant, automated content briefs, Copilot conversational search, and AI visibility tracking. Pricing starts at 117 dollars monthly for Pro billed annually (140 dollars monthly), Guru at 250 dollars, Business at 500 dollars, and Semrush One at 199 dollars. The standing criticism is cost and complexity: most users touch a fraction of the features.

Verdict: Best for one subscription covering research, content, and competitor intel.

Skip it if you only need page-level optimization tooling.

Clearscope

Clearscope is the editorial-workflow optimizer: it analyzes top-ranking pages for a target keyword and produces a brief of terms, entities, headings, and question patterns, then grades your draft against it in real time as you write. The differentiator is where it plugs in: Google Search Console and Google Docs, with a Content Decay feature that finds pages losing rankings and suggests updates based on what competitors added since your revision. Catalog AI features are content grading, keyword suggestions, draft generation, topic exploration, and content briefs, with 20 AI drafts included. Pricing is 129 dollars monthly for Essentials and 399 dollars for Business per the catalog, with custom Enterprise terms. It prices above Frase and Surfer and is bought for fit with existing editorial process; the standing criticism is exactly that price for freelancers and small teams.

Verdict: Best for teams living in Google Docs who grade while editing.

Skip it if the budget is small; comparable tools cost less.

MarketMuse

MarketMuse approaches SEO from the top down: it inventories your site, models the topics you cover, and grades your coverage against competitors across all of them instead of scoring one article at a time. Founded 2013 in Boston and acquired by Siteimprove in October 2024, it holds a patent on semantic keyword analysis and publishes metrics including Personalized Difficulty, Topic Authority, and Content Score. Generative AI is documented rather than implied: MarketMuse AI arrived in Optimize in October 2023 as an LLM-agnostic drafting layer, Content Strategy AI followed in August 2024, and briefs come in nine types from Comparison and FAQ Collection to How-to. The trade-offs are concrete: no documented API and no integrations page, WordPress publishing is copy-paste, and pricing is demo-gated with limits published instead of dollars (Free allows 10 queries a month).

Verdict: Best for site-wide content strategy and topical coverage gaps.

Skip it if you need an API or self-serve pricing today.

Superlines

Superlines is the visibility-side pick: an AI search intelligence platform that measures brand visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, tracking 10-plus engines in total. Its stated differentiator is data collection from the real user-facing AI interfaces rather than API outputs, on the argument that the two can diverge; the practical claim is visibility data matching the answers buyers get. Everything is exposed through a UI, an API, and an MCP server, and an Action Center turns visibility gaps into tasks. Pricing starts at 79 euro monthly for three engines and 50 tracked prompts on one brand, Pro at 199 euro (150 prompts, 3 brands, site audits), Growth at 379 euro (300 prompts, 7 brands, API access), with a 7-day trial and 12 percent off annual billing. Helsinki-based, agency programs central to its go-to-market. Assess it as a GEO analytics layer.

Verdict: Best for tracking brand mentions inside AI answers at one place.

Skip it if you need briefs or page optimization features.
